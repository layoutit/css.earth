# CDS VizieR terms of use

Applies to the prepared catalogues obtained through the VizieR catalogue
access tool of the Centre de Données astronomiques de Strasbourg (CDS):

- `nebulae-planetary`: Gaia EDR3 matches to HASH Galactic planetary nebulae,
  Ali et al. 2022, RAA 22, 85013
- `snr`: Green's catalogue of Galactic supernova remnants, VizieR VII/297
- `hii-regions`: WISE Catalog of Galactic HII Regions, Anderson et al. 2014,
  VizieR J/ApJS/212/1

- Terms: https://cds.unistra.fr/vizier-org/licences_vizier.html
- These are not an SPDX open licence. VizieR data are provided for scientific
  use with the acknowledgement quoted in `NOTICE.md`, and each catalogue
  remains the intellectual property of its authors and original publisher,
  who must be cited as given there.

The recorded terms string for each of these catalogues is "CDS VizieR terms
of use". They carry no additional publisher clause. A `galaxies` catalogue,
whose terms add that commercial redistribution of the pre-2021 AAS J/AJ/144/4
table requires permission, is deliberately not included; see `SOURCE.md`.
