# NASA Exoplanet Archive — U.S. Government public domain

Applies to `exoplanets`: the NASA Exoplanet Archive Planetary Systems (`ps`)
table, rows with `default_flag = 1`.

- Terms as recorded upstream: "Public Domain (U.S. Government work)".
- Usage policy: https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/usage_policy.html
- The archive is operated by the California Institute of Technology under
  contract with NASA. Its usage policy asks that publications and derived
  products carry the acknowledgement quoted in `NOTICE.md`; cssEarth carries
  it. No NASA endorsement is implied.

Public-domain status covers the archive's data as a U.S. Government work. It
does not extend to the other catalogues in this directory, each of which has
its own terms.
