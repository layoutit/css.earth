## Bounded fitting and decoder contract

**Feasible next implementation, not completed proof:** acquire a small resolved LORRI sequence for each body, keep the pinned full source mesh and fixed scale, and fit a common mesh-to-inertial orientation/epoch phase with per-exposure pointing residuals. Use label WCS/quaternion, times, ranges and inertial Sun/observer vectors as fixed or explicitly uncertainty-bounded constraints; do not invent body-fixed latitude/longitude. Fit the source PSF/blur only where supported. Inspect multiple initial orientations and symmetry alternatives. Report sensitivity to source scale, pose, shape and background choices.

Reserve whole observations and spatially separated limb sectors before optimization. Where the coarse reconstruction does not represent craters, do not invent mesh landmarks: use independent observed contours/terminators and cross-view feature consistency. Report native-pixel residuals and competing solutions, not only a training silhouette score. Accept source pixels only where plausible retained solutions agree on the mapped surface within the declared spatial uncertainty. If that leaves insufficient identifiable surface, retain that specific region as unknown rather than fabricating attitude. This is a measured acceptance gate, not a blanket claim that missing body-fixed labels make fitting impossible.

Both selected LORRI labels specify 2,880-byte FITS records and 3,656 total records (10,529,280 bytes). Primary float image starts at one-based record 12; one-sigma calibrated-DN uncertainty at 1470; quality int16 at 2928. Planes are 1,024², sample-right/line-up. Respect FITS/PDS signed storage and the quality offset 32768; an unscaled signed read would misclassify good pixels. Quality zero is good; bits 1/2/4/8/16/32 indicate transmission/reference-calibration/CCD/saturation/missing/other defects. Reject flagged pixels and nonfinite uncertainty; retain finite low/negative signal for statistical/background handling. Calibrated DN is not I/F: the actual FITS calibration constants/exposure and solar-distance conversion must be pinned and tested before a reflectance claim.

The current Voyager/SSI decoder is insufficient for this NH product: its special FITS route is an 800² SSI I/F layout, its quality logic is SSI-specific, and default bilinear sampling rejects nonpositive values. Use an isolated NH decoder feeding the existing archived-camera geometry contract once registration is measured. Tests must cover HDU offsets, row orientation, BZERO/quality, valid dark values and independent numeric anchors. Full source-mesh ray visibility, cast-shadow rejection, source/display correspondence, bounded photometric correction and area-weighted coverage remain required; applying imagery to the 800-face display silhouette alone is inadequate.

## Proteus implementation follow-through

The body package now contains the six exact calibrated image/label inputs, three OPUS records, the archive processing document, background samples and a reproducible camera fit. [color-registration.json](../../../src/planets/proteus/source/geometry/color-registration.json) retains all fit/holdout samples, source hashes and five initialization results; [register-voyager-color.py](../../../src/planets/proteus/source/preparation/register-voyager-color.py) reproduces it from the full source mesh and pinned CK/PCK. The independent sectors give nominal RMS V/B/G = 1.18/1.34/1.64 corrected pixels. Across four additional ±2-pixel seeds, centre variation is below 0.35 pixel and the largest held-out discrepancy is 4.6 pixels. The six-pixel working envelope is not a confidence interval. Nine pixels of Manhattan inset provide at least 6.36 pixels of Euclidean separation from known invalid source boundaries.

Early experiments initialized violet and green from a brightness median, which was biased into the lit disc. Those unstable early translations were rejected. The retained procedure initializes from the principal connected disc bounding box, then uses only the declared fit sectors. Diagnostic full-source limb overlays for all three final cameras were personally inspected. This does not qualify unseen terrain or eliminate the coarse source model's errors. The closest clear-frame automatic fit remained unreliable and was not adopted.

The new `filter-color` recipe uses green/blue/violet as RGB, one camera per channel, independent measured sky offsets and three-MAD-sigma masks, common 0.10-I/F/gamma-1 display scaling and exactly unit channel gains. Source uncertainty restricts interpretation to broad patterns at roughly 40 km or coarser near the image centre. The visible content explicitly excludes true-color and calibrated-albedo claims. No high-resolution clear detail is transferred.

Validation actually completed: `node --test tests/objects/unit/proteus/*.test.mjs` passed **3/3**, including the six exact input identities, independent signed-DN/I/F anchors, actual three-camera footprint and rejection of color when violet is absent; the existing radius-cardinal anchors also passed. `python src/planets/proteus/source/preparation/register-voyager-color.py src/planets/proteus/source` independently replayed and verified the retained receipt. Initial source verification identified only locally missing baseline clear frames and the starfield. Standard acquisition then completed: 13 inputs, one intermediate and28 documents verified (42 total).
