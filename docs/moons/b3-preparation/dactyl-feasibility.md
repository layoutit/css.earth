# Dactyl: bounded historical-scene feasibility

Dactyl has a stronger published encounter-state route than Selam, but remains unqualified as a standalone body. [Belton et al. (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1006/icar.1996.0044) identify a family of J2000 state vectors at 1993-08-28 16:52:04.66 UTC in Table IV. Their observations do not select a unique orbit. The table’s numerical Dactyl rows have not yet been acquired and visually verified here; no state was entered into the application. No defensible present-day orbital phase was found.

The [original PDS index](https://sbnarchive.psi.edu/pds3/galileo/idagaspra/sbnig_0001/data/galileo_ssi/ida/idaidx.html) explicitly identifies I2278 and I2700 as high-resolution Dactyl observations. Both original 645,120-byte FITS files and 10,480-byte labels are acquired and hash-pinned in `dactyl-feasibility.json`. Direct pixel inspection confirms that I2278 resolves the visible disk, while I2700 clips the moon at the detector’s left edge and mostly records its dark side. [JPL’s image description](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia00298-dactyl-dark-side-illuminated-by-idashine/) independently confirms the 24 m/pixel sampling, clipping, and reflected illumination from Ida. Its press image shifts the frame edge and enhances the faint signal, so registration must use the original file.

The [shape paper](https://doi.org/10.1006/icar.1996.0045) supports a 1.6 × 1.4 × 1.2 km ellipsoid, with the long axis approximately toward Ida and shortest axis approximately normal to the orbital plane. Those are useful encounter constraints. The two acquired images do not yet qualify a unique attitude or its uncertainty: the second supplies a partial, faint limb, and no independent fitted projection has been accepted. The [imaging overview](https://doi.org/10.1006/icar.1996.0032) explicitly says Dactyl’s rotation rate is unknown; synchronism is an interpretation.

The native labels target Ida. Their target radii, sub-spacecraft coordinates, and zero north/scale fields must not be relabeled as Dactyl geometry. The FITS data are 8-bit raw DN with returned strips and gaps, not calibrated I/F and not a quality-masked global surface. Dark values are scientifically useful; a brightness threshold must not erase Idashine or manufacture valid coverage.

Executable continuation: acquire and verify the orbit-table rows; reconstruct and validate SSI camera pointing and distortion; fit the published ellipsoid to I2278; reserve I2700’s visible limb and an additional earlier original image for independent orientation/landmark checks; quantify uncertainty and genuine photographic footprint. A resulting body needs an explicitly historical scene capability because the application’s shared world geometry is fixed to 2026. Do not insert a 1993 position or an arbitrarily propagated orbit into that context.

The source-access failures and local original-image hashes are recorded in the JSON. No registry entry, body implementation, preparation, or browser qualification was added for Dactyl.
