## Hyperion

The baseline already uses calibrated imaging rather than a press image: eleven 1024×1024 clear-filter CISSCAL 4.0-beta I/F frames, a 4096×2048 prepared monochrome map and the Thomas 2018 14,636-vertex/29,268-plate shape. The map's 81.5% valid **equirectangular pixel fraction is not surface-area coverage**. Camera resolution in the selected frames is approximately 176 m to 1.8 km per pixel. The body is chaotically rotating: the retained September 2005 frame is a source frame, not a valid present-day phase or constant spin model. Existing illumination normalization is bounded Lunar-Lambert plus overlap gain; it does not make these pixels absolute albedo or erase cast shadows.

Pinned baseline mesh: `shape/hyperion_30k_plt.tab`, 2,195,250 bytes, SHA-256 `cf90156a297d3078375583a99c2367bfe265bc5175710772e173544a25c37002`. Source documentation: [PDS Thomas Hyperion model/camera document](https://sbnarchive.psi.edu/pds4/cassini/saturn_satellite_shape_models_V1_0/document/hyperion_document.pdf).

**First implementation candidate:** the unused closer encounter sequence. Table 1 supplies controlled subobserver/subsolar latitudes and west longitudes, camera range, north azimuth and center sample/line. Example `N1506391424` has range 16,782.2 km, subobserver 10.62° latitude / 204.06° W, north azimuth 328.60°, and published center (73.0,388.2). The [actual OPUS product response](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/opus/api/files/co-iss-n1506391424.json) resolves a public calibrated [image](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/holdings/calibrated/COISS_2xxx/COISS_2015/data/1506388291_1506396299/N1506391424_2_CALIB.IMG) and [label](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/holdings/calibrated/COISS_2xxx/COISS_2015/data/1506388291_1506396299/N1506391424_2_CALIB.LBL). Thus image availability is closed; camera registration is not yet closed. The actual native label is now inspected: **FULL**, 1024×1024, clear I/F, acquired 2005-09-26T01:35:17.096 UTC. At the controlled range this implies approximately **100.5 m/pixel**, finer than the selected baseline. This candidate does not have the SUM2 ambiguity. Its native image is 4,202,496 bytes, SHA-256 `b212dac15ff2cec9213da76d2f0a540a1d6dfb10646e727bc472db0a99e25669`; native pixels were decoded at byte 8192 (4096-byte VICAR header plus one telemetry record) and visually inspected as a detailed regional terrain crop. That inspection does not yet prove geographic registration. The existing package deliberately excluded four SUM2 closeups after ambiguous center conventions; merely adding those files again is not an upgrade.

Table 1 also lists closer `N1506391975` and `N1506393257`. Its two distinct rows with the same `N1506393778_1` identifier have different centers; that ambiguity is a blocker for that row, not permission to choose the visually convenient version. A new registration should pin the exact version/clock, preserve the camera model and solve only justified center/attitude corrections. Hold out disjoint crater/limb landmarks from the fit. Report residuals, source mesh uncertainty (best encounter region versus poorly constrained reverse side), ray occlusion, incidence/emission rejection, actual added coverage and effective source sampling. Do not call a denser output map higher measured resolution without these checks.

**Newer product route remains blocked:** Zubarev and Nadezhdina's [2025 Icarus work](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2024.116440) and [LPSC 2025 abstract 1086](https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2025/pdf/1086.pdf) describe a new 166-image control solution and products as fine as 50 m/pixel. The reference frame has +East longitude and an ellipsoid with semi-axes 180.9×129.0×102.0 km. Its geodetic height above that triaxial ellipsoid and special map projection are not the current radial height above a 135 km sphere. Crossref supplies no dataset relation; the checked author/publication and PDS directories did not yield a numerical product. Publication figures are not a verified registered raster/DEM release. This is an explicit source-access/release limit, not a statement that the dataset does not exist.

## Phoebe

The existing monochrome source is [Cassini `SP_1M_0_0_SIMP.IMG`](https://planetarydata.jpl.nasa.gov/img/data/cassini/cassini_orbiter/coiss_3001/data/images/SP_1M_0_0_SIMP.IMG), 2880×1440 at 8 pixels/degree (about 233 m/pixel for its 106.8 km reference sphere), 4,158,720 bytes, SHA-256 `e2a26482052ef9d67dd5c82df096daedc9cec0c0345c94e07f9b4461622aa69a`. Missing coverage is inferred from the connected exact-zero polar region, not a published mask; interior dark crater pixels remain observed imagery. The retained [Gaskell Q128 model](https://sbnarchive.psi.edu/pds4/non_mission/gaskell.phoebe.shape-model/data/phoebe_ver128q.tab) has 99,846 vertex rows and 196,608 triangles, 16,897,992 bytes, SHA-256 `92550ba0625255a2f91cd5d9a7b52e020fc4ba76c7ef3fd76a9728a66df697c2`. Its 147 m position RMS is a model-fit statistic, not global surface resolution. The source pole is RA 356.90°, Dec 77.88°.

**Concrete defect in the proposed earlier regional source:** `source/observations/regional-color-example-label.txt` preserves an ISIS cube for wide-angle RED observation `W1465676924`, exposure 2004-163T20:04:38.952–20:04:40.152 UTC, 1,200 ms. Its equirectangular projection uses equatorial/polar radii 108,950/101,800 m, planetocentric +East longitude, domain 180, 712.95286031371 m/pixel, and `ShapeModel = Null`. More seriously, its cached BodyRotation pole is RA **355°**, Dec **68.7°**, approximately nine degrees away from the Gaskell pole. An ellipsoid-only lookup in those projected pixels is not compatible with the current mesh. Direct draping would make a false registered map.

**Feasible substantial route:** original, calibrated regional spectral channels, with reconstructed camera geometry transformed into the Gaskell source frame. The [OPUS frame response](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/opus/api/files/co-iss-w1465676924.json) currently provides [original calibrated RED image](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/holdings/calibrated/COISS_2xxx/COISS_2004/data/1465674475_1465709620/W1465676924_2_CALIB.IMG) and [label](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/holdings/calibrated/COISS_2xxx/COISS_2004/data/1465674475_1465709620/W1465676924_2_CALIB.LBL). The actual native RED image was downloaded, decoded at the VICAR-owned 8192-byte image offset, and visually inspected: a resolved partly illuminated disk with limb/crater anchors is present. It is 4,202,496 bytes, SHA-256 `1b415df5be1fe0c363053a7ceba9cbc7629fd04d3aaaf7e2de8457788002f305`. This avoids treating the inaccessible projected index as the only acquisition route. The original SBIB example reports calibrated I/F, CL1/RED band center 647.282 nm, width 149.963 nm; these are broad instrument channels, not composition measurements or natural-color certainty.

The cached cube label identifies reconstructed CK `04161_04164ra.bc`, spacecraft SPK `200128RU_SCPSE_04036_04182.bsp`, FK `cas_v40.tf`, SCLK `cas00172.tsc`, and the single-exposure instrument/body rotation and position tables at ET 140256343.73662. WAC focal length 200.74 mm and pixel pitch 0.012 mm are present, as are boresight and distortion terms. Those metadata give a bounded camera-recovery route, but the label alone does not contain the binary quaternion/position payload. Need actual cube/table bytes or a fully pinned equivalent archive-kernel closure; also resolve the matching GRN/BL1 versions and their independent geometry. Preserve channel-to-channel timing and register each image, never assume identical pointing.

Independent anchors should include disjoint crater centers/limbs in the native calibrated frames, predicted source-shape silhouette, and published subobserver/solar geometry. A correction derived from two regions must be tested on other regions. Coverage remains regional; retain an explicit mask and untouched neutral no-data surface. Shadows and variable phase do not become albedo. If color is implemented, describe it as registered broad-band relative color with its channel/stretch/photometric policy adjacent to the active lens.

The [SBIB `/data/PDS-Phoebe/index.html` endpoint](https://sbib.psi.edu/data/PDS-Phoebe/index.html) returned HTTP 403 during this review. Alternate `/PDS-Phoebe/index.html` and HTTP routes returned a generic SproutCore application, not a product directory. These are recorded access limits. The [Fraser/Brown VIMS paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.04979) is a separate mapped spectroscopy lead; no numerical spatial release has been verified here, so a colored publication figure is not selected as a raster.

## Aegaeon

The current body is the [Hedman et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09192) Table 1 ellipsoid, **semi-axes** 0.70±0.05, 0.25±0.06 and 0.20±0.08 km, not a released detailed shape mesh. Its source grid is a neutral coordinate guide. Rendering simplification error is separate from the much larger physical axis uncertainties. The historical pole comes from the retained binary orientation model at the 2015 encounter; the displayed meridian is arbitrary and does not establish current phase.

Resolved data are demonstrably available: [2015 `N1829223369` calibrated label](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/holdings/calibrated/COISS_2xxx/COISS_2100/data/1829042482_1829225208/N1829223369_1_CALIB.LBL), 2015-12-19 at 12:32:14.886 UTC midpoint, 0.06897 km/pixel and phase 24.508°, and [2010 `N1643264167` calibrated label](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/holdings/calibrated/COISS_2xxx/COISS_2060/data/1643188892_1643282464/N1643264167_1_CALIB.LBL), 2010-01-27 around 05:32:15.161 UTC midpoint, 0.08716 km/pixel and phase 73.322°. Both have actual 1024×1024 calibrated float I/F products; the 2015 frame is CISSCAL 4.0-beta, 100 ms, FULL, CL1/CL2, lossless with no missing lines/packets. At the best nominal scale the maximum 1.4 km dimension is only about 20 pixels; the 0.4 km dimension is about six pixels before projection/PSF. This cannot support invented crater detail or global photometric structure.

**Measured registration blocker:** the [fresh OPUS 2010 metadata](https://opus.pds-rings.seti.org/opus/api/metadata/co-iss-n1643264167.json) agree closely with Hedman Table 27 in subobserver latitude (74.009° versus 74.0°), phase (73.322° versus 73.3°) and range (14,546.742 versus 14,546.9 km). But OPUS gives subobserver IAU west longitude **303.269°** and subsolar west longitude **275.531°**; the paper gives **168.5°** and **140.8°**. Their approximately 134.75° offset is not resolved by simply flipping east/west. A camera using one convention cannot be combined silently with the other's body axes. The exact orientation kernel/epoch and prime-meridian definition must be reconciled first.

Table 27 also identifies RED/GRN/BL1 frames `N1643264609`, `N1643264664`, `N1643264720`, with changing geometry and approximately 97–99 m/pixel scale. Figure 18 uses `N1643265020` as the independent published resolved portrait. These are useful identification/photometry anchors; they do not establish a spatially resolved color-composition map. A bounded native-crop test must positively identify the actual body disk, compare the paper's portrait and predicted silhouette, then report registration uncertainty in pixels relative to the tiny disk. Until it passes, retain the honest ellipsoid package and classify the mapping route as source/registration-blocked. Disk-integrated reflectance research can be a future chart capability, not a fabricated surface lens.

## Preparation boundary and evidence

The existing `prepare-archived-camera.py` supports OSIRIS and L'LORRI, not Cassini ISS; extending it without a verified Cassini model would be premature. Existing shape-camera mosaicking can be reused once an observation's exact body-frame camera is qualified. A narrowly scoped Cassini camera decoder/recipe may be needed for Phoebe's cached ISIS tables, distortion and source-frame transform. Hyperion can instead reuse its controlled-camera contract if center/summing semantics and held-out features pass.

NASA/PDS archival images and labels are the selected primary scientific inputs; preserve their original provenance, versions, calibration labels and archive terms. No publication-figure pixels are selected. Do not infer dataset redistribution rights from a publisher's text-mining license. The newer Hyperion numerical release and VIMS route require their own product/terms closure if later obtained.

Small research receipts are retained in `/tmp/moons-b3-saturn/`: `initial-fetches.json`, `archive-metadata-fetches.json`, `api-fetches.json`, `native-frame-fetches.json`, `native-display-receipt.json`, native labels and exact API responses. `hedman.pdf` is 10,228,662 bytes, SHA-256 `402cc05a6551acd50657fc7f41fa732ce32cd2a442c4e4be7a3a62c6780dd2ac`; it is a local research reference, not a redistributed package input. Each retrieved product must pass byte/hash pinning before adoption. No heavy preparation or runtime qualification was run for this source review.
