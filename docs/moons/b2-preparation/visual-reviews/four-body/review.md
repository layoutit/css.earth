## Concrete visual findings

- **Phobos has thin dotted rendered boundaries, strongest at DPR 2 with Shadows off.** `phobos-dpr2/albedo-shadows-false-details-1.png` shows a long arc across the upper third and a small polygon outline to its upper-left. Corresponding boundaries also occur in elevation and are faintly visible in the existing monochrome view. These are distinct from real source detail and the intentionally discrete scientific sampling. The exact introduced-versus-baseline attribution is unproved by this capture set.
- **Dimorphos elevation shows fine colored triangular line segments at both DPRs with Shadows off.** See `dimorphos-dpr1/elevation-shadows-false-details-1.png` and the corresponding DPR 2 image. They become less conspicuous with Shadows on and are much less visible in the slope view. The image shows a rendering limitation, not evidence of scientific discontinuities. No baseline or atlas isolation was performed for this body.
- **Deimos DPR 2 has a few thin dotted edges**, especially around the left-center and right limb in `deimos-dpr2/albedo-shadows-false-details-1.png` and elevation. These are distinct from its broad, correctly labeled gray missing-coverage graticule. They are subtler than the Phobos arc and do not open visible holes in the silhouette.
- **Moon retains visible fine pole/band lines** in normal and scientific views, most apparent at DPR 1. The separate source/geometry comparison in `docs/moons/b2-preparation/moon-seam-review/` established unchanged baseline geometry/photo bytes and the inherited oriented-band overlap limitation. This review does not claim every fine line is uniquely attributed or unavoidable.

## What the inspected views establish

- **Moon:** elevation and rock abundance have readable descriptions, color scales and units at both DPRs. Elevation shows the -12/0/12 km scale. Rock abundance shows 0/1/2+ percent and visibly explains the 2-percent display cap, modeled area fraction, source period and missing/outside-60-degree coverage. Its gray polar disk with graticule is intentional missing-data presentation and rotates coherently in the drag image. The visible-color lens remains complete, with its polar interpolation and tone-adjustment qualifications shown.
- **Phobos:** all four lens identities are clear. Relative albedo is labeled SPC relative scale, not calibrated absolute albedo; slope is 0/25/50 degrees and explains the modeled gravity assumptions. Elevation is labeled relative to the 11.1 km sphere. Both new scientific lenses remain recognizable with Shadows on and through the recorded drag. The full irregular silhouette remains visible; no missing pole cap or large blank triangle was observed.
- **Deimos:** the conservative mask is sharp and follows the same terrain regions across relative albedo, slope and elevation. The sidebar explicitly distinguishes finite solved-albedo support from a complete photographic coverage measurement. Units and scales are visible. Shadows on makes the default face very dark in both normal and scientific lenses; Shadows off permits inspection. This is an observation of appearance, not an independent verification of the epoch illumination. The mask boundary and intact irregular outline remain coherent after the drag.
- **Dimorphos:** the intact flattened encounter-era shape is visible at both DPRs. The sidebar explicitly says the surface is not a model of the altered asteroid, includes modeled terrain, and uses an arbitrary display phase. Elevation's 75 m reference sphere and -20/0/20 m scale and slope's 0/35/70 degree scale are readable. The short drag preserves a coherent surface. The default camera samples mainly smooth modeled terrain; detailed observed terrain appears near the lower limb. This set does not inspect that detailed region face-on.

The captured before/after states report the expected active body and lens, no case runtime errors, stable retained node identity, and completed context closure. Those are harness receipts, not visual inference. No native/baseline parity, compositor frame rate, complete backside coverage or zero-seam claim is made. DPR 2 full-frame images were inspected at the tool's display resize; the Phobos seam was additionally inspected in an original-pixel crop.

## Bounded Phobos atlas/source check

`phobos-atlas-pixel-check.json` pins the actual 2048 by 12800 unlit albedo atlas: 2,460,704 bytes, SHA-256 `d33bb32fc9a10d308e4a7cf015d6cda46d5823cbd675a0f97f6408f71c8e39ea`. It is fully opaque RGB, with channel minima [54,74,82]. The inspected original-pixel seam includes [55,71,74] at screenshot pixel [1453,673], below corresponding atlas channel minima. The long dark line therefore is not a black line encoded into that opaque unlit texture. The first 32 atlas tiles were also visually inspected without that dotted arc; this sample is not a full atlas visual scan.

An independent exact-coordinate edge count of the retained geometry finds 1600 faces and 2400 edges, all paired with opposite winding. The prepared scalar report records zero withheld source rows, 58 withheld texels including bleed and 3 withheld triangle-interior texels. The atlas builder fills whole raster cells opaquely and clamps scientific bleed to each retained face (`tools/objects/terrestrial-layers/radial-terrain.mjs`, lines 285-286 and 320-341). These checks narrow the observed line to mounted composition, primitive coverage, or depth/facing behavior; they do not isolate which, establish source missing coverage, or justify a renderer change without further controlled evidence.
