Rings of Neptune

Feature Middle Boundary (km) Width Optical Depth Notes
Galle 42,000 2,000 10^-4 A faint and poorly understood ring.
Le Verrier 53,200 < 100 10^-2 A narrow, axisymmetric ring.
Lassell 55,200 4,000 10^-4 A uniform, faint ring extending outward from Le Verrier.
Arago 57,200     A brightness enhancement at the outer edge of Lassell.
Unnamed 61,953     A ring of dust in the orbit of Galatea.
Adams 62,933 15 0.01 to 0.1 A narrow ring containing the arcs. It shows a radial wiggles due to perturbations from nearby Galatea.
