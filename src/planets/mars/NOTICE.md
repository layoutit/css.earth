# Mars source notice

This adapter combines prepared material derived from the following sources:

- OpenSpace scene configuration and moon models, MIT licensed by the
  OpenSpace Team. The pinned license text is included as
  `LICENSE.OPENSPACE-MIT`.
- USGS Astrogeology and NASA/PDS Mars surface, MOLA, and THEMIS products.
  These United States government data products are credited in `SOURCE.md`
  and `source/manifest.json`.
- JPL Solar System Dynamics physical and orbital tables.
- NASA GSFC Planetary Spectrum Generator output and NASA Science editorial
  information.
- The HYG v4.1 registration subset is by David Nash/Astronexus under
  CC-BY-SA-4.0. The ESO `eso0932a` full-sky panorama is credited to
  ESO/S. Brunier under CC-BY-4.0. Their checked license texts are retained in
  `source/stars/`.
- Google Earth Pro camera, sky-motion, and Sun-placement measurements are used
  only as a behavioral oracle. No Google sky, shader, or Sun pixels are shipped.
  The six prepared faces use the credited ESO panorama, and the separate Sun is
  a repository-authored clean-room raster. The factual oracle contract and its
  qualification are recorded in `source/sky/google-earth-pro-contract.json`.
- [Mars in opposition 2016](https://esahubble.org/images/heic1609a/), released
  by ESA/Hubble under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The prepared
  navigation marker is cropped and resized. Credit: NASA, ESA, the Hubble
  Heritage Team (STScI/AURA), J. Bell (ASU), and M. Wolff (Space Science
  Institute).
