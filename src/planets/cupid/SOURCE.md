# Cupid source qualification

Status: research only; no standalone shape scene.

[French & Showalter (2012), section 2.2 and Table 1](https://www.rfrench.org/static/papers/French-2012-Cupid-Is-Doomed-preprint.pdf)
use a 9 ± 2 km photometric radius with an assumed albedo. This is a size estimate,
not measured three-dimensional axes. The [PDS table](https://pds-rings.seti.org/uranus/uranus_tables.html)
also provides a mean radius, not a shape reconstruction.

The [NASA Webb 2025 release](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/webb/2025/08/19/new-moon-discovered-orbiting-uranus-using-nasas-webb-telescope/)
shows Cupid as a point of light; it does not supply a resolved outline or surface map.
No qualified shape was found in the reviewed releases. Neither a spherical scene
nor a photographic surface lens is included. Target NAIF 727, parent Uranus.
