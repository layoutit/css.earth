# Sycorax source model

Sycorax is an outer moon of Uranus with measured rotational variability and thermal-size constraints. This ellipsoid adopts the minimum elongation under uniform reflectivity; depth and display orientation remain assumptions.

Approximate minimum-elongation ellipsoid using K 2 variability and a 157 (+23/−15)km thermophysical diameter. Equal short axes, volume normalization and orientation are assumed. The grid marks unmapped terrain.
