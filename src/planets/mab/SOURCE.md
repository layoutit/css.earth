# Mab source qualification

Status: research only; no standalone shape scene.

[Molter, de Pater & Moeckel (2023), sections 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.13773v1)
report an unresolved Keck detection. Photometry favors a roughly 6 km radius for
an icy, reflective surface; a roughly 12 km radius under a darker-surface
assumption remains possible. Neither scenario determines the axes. The authors
say further light-curve observations are needed for trustworthy shape constraints.

[Hedman et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.18650v2) adds JWST evidence for
an unusually blue infrared spectrum, but no resolved shape or spatial composition
map. These are useful physical constraints, not terrain or a measured sphere.
No standalone scene or mapped lens is included. Target NAIF 726, parent Uranus.
