true color mosaic

True color mosaic created from FC images calibrated and photometrically corrected according to [1,2].

True color was achieved by scaling the FC red, green, and blue mosaics to sRGB values calculated from the color matching functions defined by the International Commission on Illumination (CIE) multiplied with a Vesta spectrum [3].

Due to the large albedo variations over the surface it was necessary to display some very bright terrains as white.

  • red channel = filter #7 (650 nm)
  • green channel = filter #2 (550 nm)
  • Blue channel = filter #8 (430 nm)

  • [1] Schröder et al. (2013) "In-flight calibration of the Dawn Framing Camera", Icarus 226, 1304-1317
  • [2] Schröder et al., "In-flight calibration of the Dawn Framing Camera II: Flat fields and stray light correction", submitted to Icarus
  • [3] Xu, S. et al. (1995) "Small Main-Belt Asteroid Spectroscopic Survey: Initial Results", Icarus 115, 1-35

  • release date: 31-Mar-2014
