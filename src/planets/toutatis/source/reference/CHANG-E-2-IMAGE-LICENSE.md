# Chang’e-2 photograph

Y. Jiang, J. Ji, J. Huang, S. Marchi, Y. Li and W.-H. Ip (2015),
*Boulders on asteroid Toutatis as observed by Chang’e-2*, Scientific Reports 5,
16029. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep16029) ·
[Open-access article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4629198/) ·
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Checked 2026-09-13. The article's license includes its images unless an image
has a separate exclusion. Figure 1 has no such exclusion. The photograph used
here is panel c of that figure. The publisher's original JPEG is pinned in
the [manifest](../manifest.json); acquisition restores those exact bytes.

Changes: crop panel c, exclude annotations and uncertain edge regions, resample
RGB, approximately project the photograph onto a simplified radar mesh and
encode WebP display assets. Original display colors and illumination are
retained. This is not a calibrated surface map, and the authors do not endorse
the application. See the [method and limits](chang-e-2-method.md).

The figures from Zou et al. (2014) used for research have separate publisher
terms. They are not included in this texture or redistributed with this package.
