# Perdita source qualification

Status: research only; no standalone shape scene.

[Molter, de Pater & Moeckel (2023), sections 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.13773v1)
uses a photometric radius of 15 ± 3 km under assumed Portia-group reflectivity.
Its Keck detection is unresolved: the approximately 30 km body is much smaller
than the stated resolution element. The [released data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7689583)
provides observational images, not a three-dimensional shape or registered terrain.

[NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/moons/perdita/) describes the shape as unknown.
No measured axes or qualified reconstruction was found in the reviewed sources.
An assumed equilibrium sphere is not an observed shape. No standalone scene or
mapped lens is included. Target NAIF 725, parent Uranus.
