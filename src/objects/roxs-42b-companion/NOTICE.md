# ROXs 42B B credits

Orbit: Inglis et al. (2026), AJ 171, 280. Radius and temperature: Inglis et al. (2024), AJ. Mass: Kraus et al. (2014), ApJ 781, 20. B is placed by its orbit around ROXs 42B A, whose placement is Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1).

Colour: a Planck spectrum at the published temperature, weighted by the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0). No image of the star's surface exists.
