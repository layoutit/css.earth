# Epsilon Indi Ab credits

Orbit: the Octofitter posterior of Sanghi et al. (2026), AJ, published on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.19931118) under CC BY 4.0, and their astrometry (Table 2). Discovery: Matthews et al. (2024), Nature 633, 789. Radius, temperature and mass: Sanghi et al. (2026).

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Placement: its star at Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Sanghi et al. (2026), arXiv:2603.08787, with the MIRI fluxes of Matthews et al. (2024), Nature 633, 789 (F1550C 15.51 µm, F1065C 10.55 µm, F430M 4.278 µm).

Limb darkening: computed with PICASO 4.1 (Batalha et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 70) on Sonora Elf Owl cloud-free (Mukherjee et al. 2024, ApJ 963, 73; [M/H] +0.7, C/O 2.5 times solar, log Kzz 4) profiles (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10381250) and PICASO's resampled opacity database (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3759675). Model fit: Sanghi et al. (2026), arXiv:2603.08787.
