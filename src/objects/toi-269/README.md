# TOI-269

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Cointepas et al. 2021. This account was drafted from Cointepas et al. 2021's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 4770828304936109056, parallax 17.572 ± 0.011 mas (56.91 pc). Radius 0.398 +/- 0.012 solar radii from Cointepas et al. 2021, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of TOI-269 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2021A&A...650A.145C/abstract). Mass 0.3917 +/- 0.0095 solar masses from Cointepas et al. 2021, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of TOI-269 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2021A&A...650A.145C/abstract). Temperature 3,514 K from Cointepas et al. 2021, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of TOI-269 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.83 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 4770828304936109056, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc384. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,514 K and log g 4.83 (u1 0.394, u2 0.377): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
