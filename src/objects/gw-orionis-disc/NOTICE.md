# Credits and release terms

The 1.3 millimeter continuum image is the reduction Kraus et al. deposited with their paper, [SourceForge project aba4633](https://sourceforge.net/projects/aba4633/), file `ALMA/kraus.gwori.ALMA.fits`. Image credit: **ALMA (ESO/NAOJ/NRAO), Kraus et al.**

The deposit states no license; the source record uses `LicenseRef-No-License-Stated`. The paper’s data statement names the deposit as where its reduced data are available, and says the observations themselves are in the ALMA Science Archive under project codes **ADS/JAO.ALMA#2012.1.00496.S** and **#2018.1.00813.S**.

The ring planes, the inner ring’s sky position and height, and the brightness color scale are cited to [Kraus et al. (2020), Science 369, 1233](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aba4633): Table S6, Figure S10 and Figure 1A. The display is derived from measured FITS brightness and the publication’s color bar; its third axis is the paper’s planes, not a measurement of depth.
