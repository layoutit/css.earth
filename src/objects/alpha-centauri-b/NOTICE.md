# Alpha Centauri B credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Kervella, Bigot, Gallenne and Thévenin, "The radii and limb darkenings of α Centauri A and B", A&A 597, A137 (2017), arXiv:1610.06185. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
