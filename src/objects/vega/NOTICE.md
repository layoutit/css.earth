# Vega credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Monnier, Che, Zhao et al., "Resolving Vega and the inclination controversy with CHARA/MIRC", ApJ 761, L3 (2012), arXiv:1211.6055. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
