# WASP-39 credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from L. Mancini et al., "The GAPS programme with HARPS-N at TNG XVI: Measurement of the Rossiter-McLaughlin effect of transiting planetary systems HAT-P-3, HAT-P-12, HAT-P-22, WASP-39 and WASP-60", A&A 613, A41 (2018), arXiv:1802.03859. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
