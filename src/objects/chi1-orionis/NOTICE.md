Magnetic and brightness maps: the Zeeman-Doppler imaging maps of Willamo, T., Lehtinen, J. J., Hackman, T., et al. (2022), "Zeeman-Doppler imaging of five young solar-type stars", A&A 659, A71, doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202142022, from their VizieR deposit J/A+A/659/A71 (CDS), read unchanged.
