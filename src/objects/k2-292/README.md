**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,725 K and log g 4.36 (u1 0.469, u2 0.254): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
