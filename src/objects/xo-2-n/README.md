# XO-2 N

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Ruggieri et al. 2024. This account was drafted from Ruggieri et al. 2024's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 934346809278715776, parallax 6.659 ± 0.016 mas (150.18 pc). Radius 0.998 +/- 0.033 solar radii from Ruggieri et al. 2024, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of XO-2 N b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024A&A...684A.116R/abstract). Mass 0.96 +/- 0.05 solar masses from Ruggieri et al. 2024, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of XO-2 N b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024A&A...684A.116R/abstract). Temperature 5,290 K from Ruggieri et al. 2024, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of XO-2 N b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.42 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 934346809278715776, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffe6d5. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,290 K and log g 4.42 (u1 0.575, u2 0.183): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
