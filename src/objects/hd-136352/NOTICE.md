# HD 136352 credits

Colour: a Planck spectrum at the temperature of Delrez et al. 2021, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of HD 136352 c in the NASA Exoplanet Archive, through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75.
