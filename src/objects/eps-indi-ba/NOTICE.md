# Epsilon Indi Ba credits

Placement: Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1); this work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Proper motion, mass and temperature: Chen et al. (2022). Radius: King et al. (2010), A&A 510, A99. Distance and radial velocity: Epsilon Indi A's (see that package).

Shape: a sphere of the model radius in the shared neutral gray; no image or colour of its surface is used.
