# DH Tau credits

Placement: Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1). This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Radial velocity: Jönsson et al. (2020), AJ 160, 120 (APOGEE DR16), via SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.

Radius: Herczeg & Hillenbrand (2014), ApJ 786, 97, via VizieR J/ApJ/786/97. Temperature: Xuan et al. (2024). Mass: Kraus & Hillenbrand (2009), as adopted by Bowler et al. (2020), AJ, doi:10.3847/1538-3881/ab5b11.

Colour: a Planck spectrum at the published temperature, weighted by the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0). No image of the star's surface exists.
