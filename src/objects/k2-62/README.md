Its radius and temperature follow Howard et al. 2025. This account was drafted from Howard et al. 2025's values; the sections below are the data's own.

Radius 0.696 +/- 0.023 solar radii from Howard et al. 2025, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of K2-62 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025ApJS..278...52H/abstract). Mass 0.67 +/- 0.03 solar masses from Howard et al. 2025, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of K2-62 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025ApJS..278...52H/abstract). Temperature 4,455 K from Howard et al. 2025, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of K2-62 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.58 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** A Planck spectrum at 4,455 K, because no archive holds a spectrum of this star (stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number), through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffddba. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; planck: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 4,455 K and log g 4.58 (u1 0.775, u2 0.020): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.

- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
