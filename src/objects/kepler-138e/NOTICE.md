# Kepler-138 e credits

Radius: the NASA Exoplanet Archive's calculated radius (its Chen & Kipping 2017 mass-radius relationship): a model, not a measurement, since Kepler-138 e does not transit, via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Piaulet et al. 2023, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2023NatAs...7..206P), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Piaulet et al. 2023 (2023NatAs...7..206P), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.
