# LHS 1815

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Gan et al. 2020. It is also HIP 28754. This account was drafted from Gan et al. 2020's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 5500061456275483776, parallax 33.560 ± 0.014 mas (29.80 pc). Radius 0.501 +/- 0.03 solar radii from Gan et al. 2020, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of LHS 1815 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020AJ....159..160G/abstract). Mass 0.502 +/- 0.015 solar masses from Gan et al. 2020, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of LHS 1815 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020AJ....159..160G/abstract). Temperature 3,643 K from Gan et al. 2020, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of LHS 1815 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.74 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 5500061456275483776, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc88f. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,643 K and log g 4.74 (u1 0.399, u2 0.362): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
