# TOI-6000

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025. This account was drafted from Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2261062378812852992, parallax 13.140 ± 0.017 mas (76.10 pc). Radius 0.38 +/- 0.01 solar radii from Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of TOI-6000 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025AJ....170..148G/abstract). Mass 0.37 +/- 0.02 solar masses from Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of TOI-6000 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025AJ....170..148G/abstract). Temperature 3,419 K from Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of TOI-6000 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.85 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2261062378812852992, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc988. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret (2017), A&A 600, A30 computes from PHOENIX model atmospheres for the TESS band at 3,419 K and log g 4.85 (u1 0.165, u2 0.440): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
