# HAT-P-32 credits

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 1.367 +/- 0.031 solar radii from Wang et al. 2019, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of HAT-P-32 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019AJ....157...82W/abstract); Mass 1.132 +/- 0.051 solar masses from Wang et al. 2019, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of HAT-P-32 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019AJ....157...82W/abstract); temperature from Wang et al. 2019, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of HAT-P-32 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a Planck spectrum at the temperature of Wang et al. 2019, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of HAT-P-32 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75.

Placement: Gaia DR3 source 356348286886230272: position, parallax, proper motion and radial velocity. This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Identifiers: SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.
