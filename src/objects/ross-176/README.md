# Ross 176

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Geraldía-González et al. 2025. This account was drafted from Geraldía-González et al. 2025's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2083391813854073856, parallax 21.505 ± 0.011 mas (46.50 pc). Radius 0.569 +/- 0.02 solar radii from Geraldía-González et al. 2025, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of Ross 176 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025A&A...700A.216G/abstract). Mass 0.577 +/- 0.024 solar masses from Geraldía-González et al. 2025, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of Ross 176 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025A&A...700A.216G/abstract). Temperature 4,041 K from Geraldía-González et al. 2025, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of Ross 176 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.69 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2083391813854073856, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffbf8f. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 4,041 K and log g 4.69 (u1 0.565, u2 0.199): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
