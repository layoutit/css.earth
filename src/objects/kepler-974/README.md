Its radius and temperature follow Rappaport et al. 2013. This account was drafted from Rappaport et al. 2013's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2103377980301947264, parallax 7.491 ± 0.015 mas (133.50 pc). Radius 0.45 +/- 0.08 solar radii from Rappaport et al. 2013, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of KOI-1843.03 in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2013ApJ...773L..15R/abstract). Mass 0.46 +/- 0.08 solar masses from Rappaport et al. 2013, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of KOI-1843.03 in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2013ApJ...773L..15R/abstract). Temperature 3,584 K from Rappaport et al. 2013, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of KOI-1843.03 in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.79 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2103377980301947264, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc387. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,584 K and log g 4.79 (u1 0.394, u2 0.371): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
