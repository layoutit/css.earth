GM from the mass 0.85 Jupiter masses (Gressier et al. 2023, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2023A&A...672A..34G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive, https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023A&A...672A..34G/abstract) times JPL's Jupiter GM. A sphere: no oblateness is measured.
