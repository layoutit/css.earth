# MASCARA-4

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Zhang et al. 2022. It is also HD 85628. This account was drafted from Zhang et al. 2022's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 5245968236116294016, parallax 5.944 ± 0.017 mas (168.25 pc). Radius 1.79 +/- 0.04 solar radii from Zhang et al. 2022, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of MASCARA-4 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022A&A...666A..47Z/abstract). Mass 1.75 +/- 0.05 solar masses from Zhang et al. 2022, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of MASCARA-4 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022A&A...666A..47Z/abstract). Temperature 7,800 K from Zhang et al. 2022, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of MASCARA-4 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.18 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 5245968236116294016, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #c8d6ff. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 85628 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 7,800 K and log g 4.18 (u1 0.285, u2 0.341): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person; their quotes are sentences of the Wikipedia article "HD 85628" (revision 1375330131), verbatim, CC BY-SA 4.0.
