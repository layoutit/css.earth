# Kepler-560B

## Sources

Kepler-560B shares its motion through space with Kepler-560, 4,569 AU away, so the two are a bound pair. Both are placed where Gaia measures them. This account was drafted from El-Badry, Rix & Heintz (2021), MNRAS 506, 2269's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2082162147537267456, parallax 8.854 ± 0.228 mas (112.94 pc); its RUWE is 17.7, so the single-star astrometry fits poorly, and the parallax is used as published. Radius 0.478 +/- 0.019 solar radii from Stassun et al. (2019), AJ 158, 138 (TIC v8.2), the radius of TIC 239275856 (VizieR IV/39/tic82) (https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ab3467). Mass 0.476 +/- 0.024 solar masses from Stassun et al. (2019), AJ 158, 138 (TIC v8.2), the mass of TIC 239275856 (VizieR IV/39/tic82) (https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ab3467). Temperature 3,545 K from Stassun et al. (2019), AJ 158, 138 (TIC v8.2), the effective temperature of TIC 239275856 (VizieR IV/39/tic82). log g 4.76 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2082162147537267456, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc283. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,545 K and log g 4.76 (u1 0.401, u2 0.369): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Not shown.** Kepler-560B's orbit around Kepler-560 is not measured; both stars are placed at their Gaia DR3 positions, which is where they are.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
