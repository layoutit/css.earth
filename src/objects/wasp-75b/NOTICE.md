# WASP-75 b credits

Radius: Gomez et al. 2013 (2013A&A...559A..36G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Gomez et al. 2013, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2013A&A...559A..36G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Gomez et al. 2013 (2013A&A...559A..36G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,397 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 4.5 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
