# NASA Deep Star Maps 2020

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/

NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
Visualization by Ernie Wright (USRA). Gaia Data Release 2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.

The Milky Way-only celestial OpenEXR omits the bright Hipparcos/Tycho stars;
faint Gaia stars remain. It is not an empty or literally star-free background.
