# 51 Eridani credits

Placement: Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1). This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Radial velocity: Gontcharov (2006), AstL 32, 759. Cross-identification and spectral type: SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.

Radius, temperature and mass: Elliott et al. (2024), PASA 41, e056, CHARA Array interferometry.

Colour: Gaia DR3 BP/RP sampled spectrum (De Angeli et al. 2023, A&A 674, A2; Montegriffo et al. 2023, A&A 674, A3), weighted by the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0). Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75. No image of the star's surface exists.
