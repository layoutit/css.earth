# 51 Eridani b credits

Orbit: Denis et al. (2026), arXiv:2602.10260, VLT/HiRISE radial velocities with published astrometry.

Radius and temperature: Madurowicz et al. (2025), AJ, doi:10.3847/1538-3881/ae1028. Mass: Elliott et al. (2024), PASA 41, e056 (model).

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Rotation: none measured; the display axis is the orbit normal.

Placement: the star 51 Eridani at its Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Rajan et al. (2017), as compiled in Best, Liu, Magnier & Dupuy (2024), The UltracoolSheet v2.1 (Zenodo); zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (MKO K 2.184 µm, MKO H 1.614 µm, MKO J 1.2417 µm).
