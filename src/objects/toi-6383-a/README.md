Its radius and temperature follow Bernab&ograve; et al. 2024. This account was drafted from Bernab&ograve; et al. 2024's values; the sections below are the data's own.

Mass 0.458 +/- 0.011 solar masses from Bernab&ograve; et al. 2024, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of TOI-6383 A b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024AJ....168..273B/abstract). Temperature 3,444 K from Bernab&ograve; et al. 2024, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of TOI-6383 A b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.78 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** A Planck spectrum at 3,444 K, because no archive holds a spectrum of this star (stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number), through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc588. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; planck: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret (2017), A&A 600, A30 computes from PHOENIX model atmospheres for the TESS band at 3,444 K and log g 4.78 (u1 0.171, u2 0.435): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
