**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 3145754895088191744, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffefe8. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 63935 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,513 K and log g 4.45 (u1 0.518, u2 0.222): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
