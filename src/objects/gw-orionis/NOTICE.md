# GW Orionis credits

Disc observations and brightness colors: ALMA (ESO/NAOJ/NRAO) and Kraus et al. (2020), Science 369, 1233, from the image the authors deposited and their Figure 1A. The [disc credits](../gw-orionis-disc/NOTICE.md) carry the deposit’s terms.

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 7.630508 solar radii from Model radius derived from Kraus et al. (2020), Table S3: L = 48 solar luminosities and T = 5500 K; R/Rsun = sqrt(48) (5772/5500)^2, using the IAU nominal solar temperature. The luminosity is the unresolved light of three stars, so this is the sphere that would give that light, not a measured stellar radius (https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aba4633); Mass 5.26 solar masses from Kraus et al. (2020), Table S5: total dynamical mass of the three stars, 5.26 +/- 0.22 solar masses, from their astrometric and radial-velocity orbits (https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aba4633); temperature from Kraus et al. (2020), Table S3: adopted effective temperature.

Color: Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 3340856533286969600, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Placement: Gaia DR3 source 3340856533286969600: position, proper motion; distance: Kraus et al. (2020), Table S3: adopted distance, 388 +/- 5 pc, the one the paper's ring radii in astronomical units assume. This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Identifiers: SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.
