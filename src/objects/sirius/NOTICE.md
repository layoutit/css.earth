# Sirius credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Kervella, Thévenin, Morel, Bordé and Di Folco, "The interferometric diameter and internal structure of Sirius A", A&A 408, 681 (2003), arXiv:astro-ph/0306604. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
