# WASP-94 A b credits

Radius: Stassun et al. 2017 (2017AJ....153..136S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Stassun et al. 2017, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2017AJ....153..136S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Stassun et al. 2017 (2017AJ....153..136S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,535 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.

Climate model: Mukherjee et al. (2025), "Cloudy mornings and clear evenings on a gas giant exoplanet" (arXiv:2505.10910), Met Office Unified Model simulation, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15085825, CC BY 4.0.
