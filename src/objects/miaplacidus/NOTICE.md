# Miaplacidus credits

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 5.93 +/- 0.26 solar radii from Computed here, not printed by a paper: 5.93 +/- 0.26 solar radii, from the limb-darkened angular diameter 1.59 +/- 0.07 mas of Hanbury Brown, Davis & Allen (1974), MNRAS 167, 121 (the Narrabri intensity interferometer; Code, Davis, Bless & Hanbury Brown (1976), ApJ 203, 417, Table 1, and the JMDC) and the Hipparcos parallax 28.82 +/- 0.11 mas (van Leeuwen 2007, XHIP) (https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/167.1.121); No mass is measured, so GM is 0, the records' unpublished value; temperature from Code, Davis, Bless & Hanbury Brown (1976), ApJ 203, 417, HD 80007: effective temperature 9240 +/- 220 K (Table 6), from the measured angular diameter and the star's flux from the ultraviolet to the infrared.
