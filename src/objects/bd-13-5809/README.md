Mass 2.902 (2.794 to 3.011) from Gaia DR3 FLAME (Creevey et al. 2023, A&A 674, A26; astrophysical_parameters of the same source). Temperature 4,821.1 K from Gaia DR3 astrophysical parameters (CDS I/355/paramp), source 6888761925104124544: Teff 4821.1 K from the BP/RP spectra (GSP-Phot). log g 2.91 from the mass and radius.

**Color.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 6888761925104124544, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffdebb. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 4,821.1 K and log g 2.91 (u1 0.684, u2 0.106): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Not shown.** M73 has no package of its own: it is a chance alignment of unrelated stars. Its three brightest are drawn as bodies; Gaia DR3 publishes no temperature or radius for the next (Gaia DR3 6888762676722910720), so it is left out.
