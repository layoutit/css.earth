# Aldebaran credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Richichi and Roccatagliata, "Aldebaran’s angular diameter: How well do we know it?", A&A 433, 305 (2005), arXiv:astro-ph/0502181. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
