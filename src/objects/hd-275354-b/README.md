**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,109.7 K and log g 4.47 (u1 0.625, u2 0.144): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Not shown.** HD 275354B's orbit around TOI-6038 A is not measured; both stars are placed at their Gaia DR3 positions, which is where they are.
