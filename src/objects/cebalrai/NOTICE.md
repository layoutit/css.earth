(2018), AJ 155, 30, HD 161096: radius 12.17 +/- 0.06 solar radii (Table 5), from the limb-darkened angular diameter 4.511 +/- 0.011 mas (NPOI, Table 4) and the Hipparcos (van Leeuwen 2007) parallax (https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/aa9d8b); Mass 1.44 +/- 0.16 solar masses from Baines et al.
