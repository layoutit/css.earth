# K2-18 credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Sarkis, Henning, Kürster et al., "The CARMENES search for exoplanets around M dwarfs: A low-mass planet in the temperate zone of the nearby K2-18", AJ 155, 257 (2018), arXiv:1805.00830. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
