Global Thermal Inertia map from OTES data measured during all 7 Equatorial Stations.
OTES data were used to derive this product.
Map was created using the ATPM thermophysical model.
The mapping algorithm used is averaging (avrg).
Spot radiance spectra were fit directly (rather than fitting derived temperatures).
Bond albedo was assumed to be constant across the surface.
Sub-facet surface roughness was fit simultaneously with thermal inertia.
Includes variable scale factor to optimize fitting of the shape of radiance spectra.
Actual uncertainties are not strictly symmetric or Gaussian.
anc6 version provides average 1-sigma uncertainties.
anc10 version provides upper and lower 1- and 3-sigma uncertainties.
This is the version published in Rozitis et al. (2020; Science Advances)
