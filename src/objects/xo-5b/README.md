# XO-5 b

## Sources

It is the only planet known around Absolutno. Its orbit and size follow Smith 2015's fit, the archive's default. The introduction is generated from Smith 2015's published values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Size and mass.** Radius 1.14 Jupiter radii from Smith 2015 (2015AcA....65..117S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015AcA....65..117S/abstract): 81,500.9 km at 71,492 km per Jupiter radius. GM from the mass 1.19 Jupiter masses (Smith 2015, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2015AcA....65..117S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive, https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015AcA....65..117S/abstract) times JPL's Jupiter GM. A sphere: no oblateness is measured.

**Orbit.** Kokori et al. 2023 (2023ApJS..265....4K), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname KOKORI_ET_AL__2023): P 4.18775631 d Smith 2015 (2015AcA....65..117S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname SMITH_2015): a/R* derived from its semi-major axis 0.0515 au and stellar radius 1.13 solar radii; Smith 2015 (2015AcA....65..117S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname SMITH_2015): inclination 86.8 degrees Smith 2015 (2015AcA....65..117S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname SMITH_2015): e 0 Kokori et al. 2023 (2023ApJS..265....4K), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname KOKORI_ET_AL__2023): transit mid-time 2456399.47297 BJD, taken as BJD_TDB; with its period, the row that predicts 2026-01-01 best (1 sigma 1 min) Display convention: transit photometry does not measure the orbit's position angle on the sky, so the ascending node is set at position angle 0 (celestial north).

**Color.** No image or measured color exists. The neutral gray is lit by xo-5's measured color (#ffecdf, the color dataset of xo-5 (src/objects/xo-5/source/photometry/stellar-color.json)) at the gray's own brightness.

**Charts.** The orbits of Absolutno's planets from above, from their hosted-orbit records, and its transit in 2 TESS sectors (47, 60), folded onto its orbit. Upper limits and rows without an error are left out.
