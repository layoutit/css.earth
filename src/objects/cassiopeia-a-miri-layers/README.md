# Cassiopeia A, mid infrared

ESA/Webb's mid-infrared picture of the supernova remnant Cassiopeia A, as a shell. It is the Cassiopeia A page's second dataset, beside [Webb's near-infrared picture](../cassiopeia-a-layers/README.md), and it was written and baked by `telescope new-object` from one spec entry ([guide](../../../packages/telescope-cli/README.md)). **The depths are the near-infrared dataset's method, set for this picture's date: the forward shock's measured rim for the smooth light, and the ejecta where Spitzer's [Ar II] spectra measured them, with the outer knots and jets beyond the shock. The Green Monster, which two papers place in front of the remnant, is not laid there: no paper gives its outline as numbers.**

## The picture

- **Registration:** the file's embedded sky tags, used as they are: 0.1109″ per pixel and north 0.5° left of vertical. The remnant has no star to set the frame by; the page stands at its expansion centre, 14.1″ east and 16.0″ south of the frame's centre.
- **Date:** the mosaic was taken on 2022 August 4 and 5, three months before the NIRCam picture. Every free-expansion scale is counted from 1671.3 to 2022.59: the rim is grown 1.0619 from its 2002.10 radii (the NIRCam bank's 1.0627), and the depth table is the NIRCam bank's shrunk by 0.9993, its outer knots grown 1.0399 and its jets 1.0015 from their own epochs. The gap-filling pointings of October are drawn at the same date: 0.06% of a radius, 0.1″.
- **What the colors show:** by the release, red is 25.5 µm, orange-red 21, orange 18, yellow 12.8, green 11.3, cyan 10, light blue 7.7 and blue 5.6 µm. By the survey paper's Table 1 every filter holds warm dust, with [Mg V] at 5.6, [Ar II] and PAHs at 7.7, [Ar III] and [S IV] at 10, PAHs at 11.3, [Ne II] and [Ne V] at 12.8, [Fe II] and [S III] at 18, [S III] at 21 and [O IV] at 25.5 µm. The orange curtains at the rim are dust where the blast wave meets gas the star had shed; the pink filaments inside are the star's own material; the green loop across the middle is the Green Monster.
- **The depths:** the near-infrared dataset's method; [its README](../cassiopeia-a-layers/README.md) has it. Where the model has [Ar II] ejecta within about 9″, the picture's fine detail lies in front of the picture's plane at the depth of the approaching ejecta there and behind it at the depth of the receding ones. Beyond the forward shock the outer optical knots and jets are two surfaces of their own (`columns.surface`): their share of a place needs one of their rows within about 9″, their depth is their rows' mean within about 13.5″, and there the smooth light stays on the plane (`beyondRing`). The geometry is the same remnant's, so the table and its surfaces are the NIRCam bank's, set for this date.
- **The smooth light:** the coarse scales of the picture's starlet decomposition, to about 32 px, with the filaments the measurements place taken out (`glow: "starlet"`), as in the near-infrared bank. It lies on the forward shock, a sphere of 153″ shaped in each direction to the measured rim (DeLaney & Rudnick 2003, the outer envelope of 10 of their 29 filaments, grown to this date), and from 0.7 of the rim outward it is handed to the plane (`lightJoinsFrom`). The halves share its broad part, and the half behind the plane holds the rest. In this picture it is most of the light: the dust.
- **The line the model was measured in is in this picture:** the 7.7 µm filter holds [Ar II] 6.99 µm. The other filters' lines and the dust take the same depths; no other line's speeds are measured here.
- **Stars:** the picture's stars are not the remnant's. [NOX](../../../labs/nebula/docs/star-removal.md) removes them before the bake (`node labs/nebula/run.mts remove-stars src/objects/cassiopeia-a-miri-layers --star-red-over-blue=0.35 --star-green-over-blue=0.8 --star-most-pixels=5000 --spike-lines=4`). At mid infrared a star is bluest: its light falls with wavelength, and the remnant's lines and dust fill the greener and redder filters. Plain NOX took the bright ring's knots, green and white, with the stars, and blurred the ring. So the picture keeps its own pixels where the light NOX took is redder than 0.35 of its blue or greener than 0.8 of it, or where its patch joins more than 5,000 px, which NOX's patches do only across the bright ring ([star-color.ts](../../../labs/nebula/packages/reconstruction/src/star-removal/star-color.ts)). Measured on plain NOX's patches: its small ones (under 150 px) hold 29% of their light at red under 0.3 of blue and green under 0.7; the field's bright stars are at red 0.14 to 0.24 of blue and green 0.54 to 0.59; the ring's large patches at red 0.32 to 0.43 and green 0.85 to 0.90. With the limits, 5,692 of 15,438 patches are stars, and 98% of the light taken has red under half its blue. MIRI's bright stars carry spikes on lines the picture measures at 9°, 34°, 135° and 156°; `--spike-lines=4` takes 2 of them out. With 2 lines it found 9, and one of them was a white knot of the ring, cut to a dark hole. Presentation choices, every one.
- **Sky:** the mosaic holds no empty sky: faint interstellar dust lies across it. Its sky is 14 of 255 levels in its brightest channel, the median of the emptiest 1% of its 64-px tiles inside the filled part (18 of 1,819, 190″ to 225″ north-north-west of the expansion centre), and 17 at their pixels' 90th percentile; the recipe's floor, 0.067, takes that away. The near-infrared bank's rule, the 90th percentile of the emptiest 5% of tiles, would be 30 here: the tiles' medians climb from 15 at 1% to 31 at 5%, which is the dust. The floor takes 0.3% of the light above the sky; inside 150″ of the expansion centre 0.03% of the pixels are that dark, and the remnant's dark lanes stand at 45 levels and more (its 1st percentile inside 120″).
- **Drawing:** the surfaces are meshes of flat patches, as in the near-infrared bank: 1,055 patches. The face is 1,500 px of the picture's 4,008.
- **Size:** 7.41 × 7.41 arcmin, 7.33 pc wide at 3,400 pc; the forward shock's sphere is 5.04 pc across.
- **Edge of the picture:** the published picture is a square mosaic turned about 45° in its frame: half the frame is empty. The filled part's corners (`fadeOutline.filledCornersPixels`) are where straight lines fitted to the edges of its light meet, at 2 to 3 px rms; the bake takes that square for the frame ([`fade-outline.ts`](../../../packages/bake/src/image-layers/fade-outline.ts), [`prepare.ts`](../../../packages/bake/src/image-layers/prepare.ts)). The picture is drawn to the near-infrared bank's boundary (`geometry.fadeAt: "outline"`): in each direction the measured rim pushed out by 1.25, or as far as the outer knots and jets reach, rounded over 10° either side; where the mosaic's edge is nearer, that edge 12″ inside, its corners cut by 20″ arcs; faded over its inner 20″. The mosaic's edge lies 143″ to 236″ from the expansion centre, nearer than the rim pushed out in every direction, so here the boundary is the mosaic's edge less its margin. Of the light above the sky (2026-10-09, `output/cas-a-miri/after-diagnostic.png`, by 5° directions) 87.1% is drawn: 6.8% lies past the boundary and 6.0% in its feather. By quarter: north 84% to 96%, east 81%, south 88%, west 88%. The cloud runs off the mosaic on every side, most to the east and the west. The old circle of 142.8″ drew 69.9%.
- **Far view:** from afar, while another body is selected, the bank is one image on a plane fixed in its frame, drawn as the Milky Way's backing is. [`prepare-galaxy-backing.mts`](../../../packages/bake/cli/prepare-galaxy-backing.mts) reads the [backing recipe](source/backing/recipe.json) and composites the bank's flat source-facing slices as seen from the Sun, the picture its camera-facing billboard drew, onto the slices' own plane through the frame's centre. The plane therefore lies where the layered model's picture plane does, and turns and foreshortens as the model does. After a rebake of the bank, run `node packages/bake/cli/prepare-galaxy-backing.mts src/objects/cassiopeia-a-miri-layers backing` again. From afar Cassiopeia A is drawn by one of its banks at a time: the bank of its selected dataset, or its default bank, [`cassiopeia-a-layers`](../cassiopeia-a-layers/README.md), while none is selected. The others draw nothing there, so two far images of the same nebula never overlap.

## Known problems

- The far picture holds only the flat slices' light, and this bank's light is almost all on its walls, which are left out, as they were from the billboard it replaced: from afar the plane draws almost nothing. Edge-on it vanishes.
- The Green Monster is on the wrong side. X-ray spectra put it in front of the remnant (Vink et al. 2024; De Looze et al. 2024), but no paper gives its outline as numbers, so it is not treated apart: its broad light is shared by the sphere's two halves and the rest of it lies on the half behind the picture's plane.
- The depths are measured in one line, [Ar II]. Dust, which is most of this picture's light, and the other lines are drawn at those depths or on the shock's sphere.
- The mosaic stops short of the remnant's outer dust and knots on every side, and the picture ends 12″ inside its edge: 87% of the light above the sky is drawn, 81% to the east.
- The sky floor keeps the faint interstellar dust across the mosaic; outside the forward shock it lies on the picture's plane with the remnant's own outer light, not told apart.
- Stars on the bright ring and on the Green Monster, whose patches join the gas, stay, and so does the brightest star on the Green Monster with its spikes. The two spiked stars taken out leave a dim cross with a dark middle, and some removed stars a faint dark ring. Ejecta as blue as a star, red under 0.35 of blue and green under 0.8, can go with the stars.
- Measured patches that rise more than 3″ in depth an arcsecond across, at least 8 px wide (2026-10-09, the bank before against this one): inside, 47 of 510 up to 9.3, and 39 of 566 up to 11.7; near the rim, where the circle drew none, 5 of 107 up to 3.5; beyond it 0 of 23, up to 1.8. 14 patches near the rim stand more than 20″ off the plane with no row within one reach. The sphere's patches near its outline are steep by its shape, 70 of 359.
- Everything the [near-infrared dataset's known problems](../cassiopeia-a-layers/README.md#known-problems) say of the depths and the meshes holds here.
- The picture is 0.11″ a pixel and is drawn at 1,500 px, 0.30″ a pixel. The near-infrared bank draws 0.13″.
- Colors are the publisher's display composite, not a measurement.
- The bank is 1.7 MB of images and 1.9 MB of leaf records (0.3 MB compressed), in 1,056 leaves. Headless Chromium draws it; frames are not timed, and Safari is not measured yet.
