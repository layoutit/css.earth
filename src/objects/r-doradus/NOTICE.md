# R Doradus credits

Image: ALMA project 2022.1.01071.S (PI T. Khouri), band 7 continuum, member OUS `uid://A001/X35f5/Xaea`, observed 18 July
2023 and public since 23 August 2024. Imaged by the ALMA pipeline and served by the ALMA Science Archive; the observations are
published in Vlemmings et al. (2024, *Nature* 633, 323). This paper makes use of the following ALMA data:
ADS/JAO.ALMA#2022.1.01071.S. ALMA is a partnership of ESO (representing its member states), NSF (USA) and NINS (Japan),

Measurements: distance and proper motion from the revised Hipparcos reduction (van Leeuwen 2007, *A&A* 474, 653) through
SIMBAD; radial velocity from the General Catalogue of Stellar Radial Velocities (Wilson 1953) through SIMBAD; the 338 GHz
diameter, stellar mass, effective temperature and convective cell size from Vlemmings et al. (2024); the infrared diameter
from Ohnaka et al. (2019, *ApJ* 883, 89); the 1.25 micrometre uniform disc from Bedding et al. (1997, *MNRAS* 286, 957)
through the JMMC JMDC. SIMBAD and VizieR are operated at CDS, Strasbourg.
