# Mathilde notices

NEAR MSI observations and Thomas shape data: NASA, JHUAPL, P. C. Thomas and colleagues; archived by NASA PDS Small Bodies Node. Preserve original labels and attribution.

Native MSI close-ups: NEAR MSI team, JHUAPL and NASA; NASA PDS Small Bodies Node, `near.msi` V1.0, DOI 10.26033/kebb-xk05. The native image and raw-product LIDVIDs are preserved in the source catalogue. Thomas reconstructed image geometry is `urn:nasa:pds:ast-sat.thomas.shape-models:data:253mathimg_tab::1.0`. These PDS data are reused with attribution under the archive’s [data-use policy](https://pds.nasa.gov/datastandards/data-use-policy.shtml). Calibration values cite Murchie et al. (1999), DOI 10.1006/icar.1999.6118; no journal figures or paper text are redistributed.

Published visualization shape: Stooke, P. (2025), *Stooke small bodies shape models V1.0*, urn:nasa:pds:small_bodies.stooke.shape-models::1.0, NASA Planetary Data System, DOI 10.26033/yt84-5y91. The archive documents aesthetic modifications to unseen regions and shadowed crater floors. Reuse here does not convert those modeled regions into observations.

Photomosaic: P. Stooke and J. Pfau, with positional control by P. Thomas; NEAR images. Citation: Stooke, P., *Stooke Small Bodies Maps V3.0*, MULTI-SA-MULTI-6-STOOKEMAPS-V3.0, NASA Planetary Data System, 2015. The pinned map guide states that the maps are public domain with proper credit, intended for visualization, and unsuitable for photometric analysis.

Physical/orbital data: NASA/JPL Horizons.

README.md records coordinate conventions, partial coverage, modeled geometry, illumination limits and surveyed alternatives. Research-paper PDFs are not redistributed as body-package sources.

Feature names, centres, diameters, extents and name origins are from the Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature, maintained by the USGS Astrogeology Science Center for the IAU Working Group for Planetary System Nomenclature. The archived export is a United States Government work in the public domain; see `source/features/manifest.json`.

Feature caption notes: 4 lead summaries from the English Wikipedia (Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0), joined to the Gazetteer through Wikidata (CC0); each note links its article in `source/features/notes.json`.
