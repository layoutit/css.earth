## Known problems

- **Orbit convention.** omega 57 degrees is taken as Barros et al. 2023 gives it through the archive (pl_orblper); papers differ on whether that is the star's or the planet's argument of periastron. The epoch is the transit, so a swapped convention would only mirror the ellipse about the line of sight.
