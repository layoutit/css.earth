# Proxima Centauri d credits

Radius: the NASA Exoplanet Archive's calculated radius (its Chen & Kipping 2017 mass-radius relationship): a model, not a measurement, since Proxima Centauri d does not transit. Mass: Suárez Mascareño et al. (2025), A&A, doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202553728, Table 3: minimum mass (m sin i) 0.26 +/- 0.038 Earth masses from the star's radial velocities; the same table gives 0.357 +/- 0.072 Earth masses for an orbit in the star's equatorial plane, tilted 47 degrees.

Orbit: Suárez Mascareño et al. (2025), A&A, doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202553728, Table 3, a fit of the star's radial velocities with the eccentricity fixed to zero (a/R* is its 0.02881 au over the star's recorded 98,094 km); the orbit's plane is assumed, from the star's rotation axis in Klein et al. (2021, MNRAS 500, 1844).
