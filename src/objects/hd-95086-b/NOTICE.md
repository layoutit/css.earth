# HD 95086 b credits

Orbit: the posterior of Bowler et al. (2020), AJ 159, 63, distributed by whereistheplanet (Wang et al. 2021); test positions: Desgrange et al. (2022), A&A 664, A139, Table 3. Radius, temperature and mass: Malin et al. (2024).

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Placement: its star at Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of De Rosa et al. (2016), ApJ 824, 121; zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (NaCo L′ 3.77 µm, GPI K1 2.029 µm, GPI H 1.632 µm).

Limb darkening: computed with PICASO 4.1 (Batalha et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 70) on Exo-REM cloudy (Charnay et al. 2018, ApJ 854, 172; 3.16 times solar metallicity, C/O 0.35) profiles (https://lesia.obspm.fr/exorem/YGP_grids/old_grids_2021/) and PICASO's resampled opacity database (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3759675). Model fit: Malin et al. (2024), arXiv:2408.16843.
