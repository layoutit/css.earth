**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 4783664690873269888, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffdeca. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 4,929 K and log g 4.6 (u1 0.677, u2 0.103): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
