Cite Marchis et al., A&A 653 A57 (2021), DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202140874, and Vernazza et al., A&A 654 A56 (2021), DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202141781. The associated research articles are released under CC BY 4.0. The model is supplied publicly by the source authors; retain this attribution and the original scientific citation when redistributing display derivatives.

Photograph: the same authors and survey. The SPHERE photograph view uses five deconvolved VLT/SPHERE/ZIMPOL frames from July and August 2017, https://observations.lam.fr/astero/Data/216Kleopatra/Deconv/, cast onto the survey's ADAM reconstruction, https://observations.lam.fr/astero/3Dshape/216_Kleopatra_adam.obj. The exposures were taken under ESO large programme 199.C-0074 (PI P. Vernazza). Cite the same two articles. The deconvolution is the survey's own and carries no radiometric calibration, so the displayed grayscale is photographed illumination and matched relative frame brightness, not measured albedo or colour. Retain this attribution when redistributing display derivatives.

The grid used by the Shape view is a display treatment, not an observed albedo or optical map. Source axes, model limitations and the arbitrary display rotation phase are described in SOURCE.md. The source is simplified to the authored display budget; no additional terrain is synthesized.
