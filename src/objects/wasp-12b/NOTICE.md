# WASP-12 b credits

Radius: Leonardi et al. 2024 (2024A&A...686A..84L), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Collins et al. 2017, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2017AJ....153...78C), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Leonardi et al. 2024 (2024A&A...686A..84L), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Colour: a black body at the 3,028 K dayside brightness temperature of López-Morales et al. 2010, dayside brightness temperature at 0.9 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions.

Temperature maps: drawn from the Spitzer phase-curve fits of Bell, T. J., Zhang, M., Cubillos, P. E., et al. (2019), "Mass loss from the exoplanet WASP-12b inferred from Spitzer phase curves", MNRAS 489, 1995, doi:10.1093/mnras/stz2018 (Table A2). Only the published table values are used; no figure is copied. The maps are the inversion of Cowan, N. B. & Agol, E. (2008), ApJ 678, L129, doi:10.1086/589232. This work is based in part on observations made with the Spitzer Space Telescope, operated by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, California Institute of Technology, under a contract with NASA.
