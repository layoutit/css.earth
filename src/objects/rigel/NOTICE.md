# Rigel credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Moravveji, Guinan, Shultz et al., "Asteroseismology of the nearby SN-II progenitor Rigel. Part I", ApJ 747, 108 (2012), arXiv:1201.0843. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
