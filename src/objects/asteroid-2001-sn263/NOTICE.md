# 2001 SN263 Alpha credits

Shape data: Becker et al. (2020), Shape model of Asteroid (153591) 2001 SN263 V1.0, NASA Planetary Data System, DOI [10.26033/7h7w-bx62](https://doi.org/10.26033/7h7w-bx62). Original radar and light-curve interpretation: Becker et al. (2015). PDS data reuse follows the [PDS data citation policy](https://pds.nasa.gov/citations/). The article has separate publisher terms; its figures are not runtime textures.

Orbital facts: Fang et al. (2011) and JPL Horizons. Source license documents accompany the package.
