# HD 35296 credits

Magnetic and brightness maps: the Zeeman-Doppler imaging maps of Willamo, T., Lehtinen, J. J., Hackman, T., et al. (2022), "Zeeman-Doppler imaging of five young solar-type stars", A&A 659, A71, doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202142022, from their VizieR deposit J/A+A/659/A71 (CDS), read unchanged. The spectra are ESO 3.6 m HARPSpol observations from December 2017.

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 1.1 solar radii from Waite et al. (2015), MNRAS 449, 8, as listed by Willamo et al. (2022), Table 2 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06729); Mass 1.096 (1.056 to 1.136) from Gaia DR3 FLAME (Creevey et al. 2023, A&A 674, A26; astrophysical_parameters of the same source); temperature from Casagrande et al. (2011), A&A 530, A138, as listed by Willamo et al. (2022), Table 2. Spin: Willamo et al. (2022), Table 2, the values of the ZDI inversion.

Colour: Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 3400292798990117888, through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75.

Placement: Gaia DR3 source 3400292798990117888: position, parallax, proper motion and radial velocity. This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Identifiers: SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.
