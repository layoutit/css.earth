Its radius and temperature follow Meier Valdés et al. 2025. This account was drafted from Meier Valdés et al. 2025's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2785466581298775680, parallax 40.452 ± 0.036 mas (24.72 pc); its RUWE is 1.6, so the single-star astrometry fits poorly, and the parallax is used as published. Radius 0.3714 +/- 0.01 solar radii from Meier Valdés et al. 2025, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of TOI-1468 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025A&A...698A..68M/abstract). Mass 0.3527 +/- 0.0081 solar masses from Meier Valdés et al. 2025, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of TOI-1468 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025A&A...698A..68M/abstract). Temperature 3,376 K from Meier Valdés et al. 2025, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of TOI-1468 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.85 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2785466581298775680, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffca85. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret (2017), A&A 600, A30 computes from PHOENIX model atmospheres for the TESS band at 3,376 K and log g 4.85 (u1 0.162, u2 0.447): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
