# XO-3 b credits

Radius: Rusznak et al. 2025 (2025ApJ...983L..42R), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Rusznak et al. 2025, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2025ApJ...983L..42R), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Rusznak et al. 2025 (2025ApJ...983L..42R), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,107 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 4.5 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
