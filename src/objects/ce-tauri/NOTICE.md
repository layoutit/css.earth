# CE Tauri credits

Photographs: the reconstructed images of Montargès, Norris, Chiavassa, Tessore, Lèbre and Baron, "The convective photosphere of the red supergiant CE Tauri. I. VLTI/PIONIER H-band interferometric imaging", A&A 614, A12 (2018), doi:10.1051/0004-6361/201731471, as deposited at the CDS (J/A+A/614/A12); observations with ESO/VLTI/PIONIER under programme 298.D-5005. The calibrated visibilities they are checked against are distributed by the JMMC Optical interferometry DataBase (OiDB). Cite the paper and the catalogue when reusing the images.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion and parallax from Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2020); radial velocity from Famaey et al., A&A 430, 165 (2005). Mass for the display GM: the stellar parameters of Montargès et al. (2018). No measured rotation axis: the display axis is celestial north.
