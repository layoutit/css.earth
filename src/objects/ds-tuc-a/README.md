# DS Tuc A

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Newton et al. 2019. It is also HD 222259, HIP 116748. This account was drafted from Newton et al. 2019's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 6387058411482257536, parallax 22.637 ± 0.015 mas (44.18 pc). Radius 0.964 +/- 0.029 solar radii from Newton et al. 2019, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of DS Tuc A b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019ApJ...880L..17N/abstract). Mass 1.01 +/- 0.06 solar masses from Newton et al. 2019, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of DS Tuc A b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019ApJ...880L..17N/abstract). Temperature 5,428 K from Newton et al. 2019, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of DS Tuc A b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.47 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 6387058411482257536, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffefeb. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 222259 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,428 K and log g 4.47 (u1 0.540, u2 0.207): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
