# HD 238107

## Sources

M40, also called Winnecke 4, is an optical double: this giant star and HD 238108 lie at different distances. The introduction is generated from Merrifield, Gray & Haran (2016)'s published values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 1575047353068482688, parallax 3.219 ± 0.012 mas (310.64 pc). Radius 4.378 (4.288 to 4.467) from Gaia DR3 FLAME (Creevey et al. 2023, A&A 674, A26; astrophysical_parameters of the same source). Mass 1.849 (1.761 to 1.889) from Gaia DR3 FLAME (Creevey et al. 2023, A&A 674, A26; astrophysical_parameters of the same source). Temperature 5,290.9 K from Gaia DR3 astrophysical parameters (CDS I/355/paramp), source 1575047353068482688: Teff 5290.9 K from the BP/RP spectra (GSP-Phot). log g 3.42 from the mass and radius.

**Color.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 1575047353068482688, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffe9d9. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 238107 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,290.9 K and log g 3.42 (u1 0.558, u2 0.197): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Not shown.** M40 has no package of its own: it is two unrelated stars, each drawn as its own body.
