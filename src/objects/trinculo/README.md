# Trinculo

Trinculo is an irregular moon of Uranus.

## Identity

Trinculo is Uranus XXI. JPL Horizons carries it as body 721.

## Sources

Pinned inputs are listed in the [source manifest](source/manifest.json).

Sheppard, Jewitt and Kleyna (2005), Table 3, estimate a radius of 9 km for S/2001 U1 from its R-band brightness, assuming a geometric albedo of 0.04. The size is an estimate from an assumed albedo, not a measurement. JPL's satellite discovery table identifies S/2001 U1 as Trinculo.

The orbit is a precessing ellipse fitted to JPL Horizons osculating elements sampled every 5 days from 2020 to 2032, with a position correction series, by the same shared generator as Uranus's other irregular moons.

- [Sheppard, Jewitt and Kleyna (2005), AJ 129, 518](https://doi.org/10.1086/426329): radius for an assumed albedo of 0.04.
- [JPL Horizons](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/): the orbit the fit samples.
- [JPL planetary satellite discovery data](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/discovery.html): identity and provisional designation.

## Evidence

No dated test report exists for this body yet.

## Known problems

The body is drawn as a sphere with the shared grid that marks unmapped terrain. Its true shape, pole, rotation period, colour and albedo pattern are not published, and none is shown. The pole and prime meridian are display conventions.
