# Hydra credits and reuse

- Shape: Simon Porter (2021), *Shape Model of Hydra*, version 1, DOI [10.6084/m9.figshare.12779975.v1](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.12779975.v1). NASA New Horizons Project funding. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). cssEarth simplifies the original mesh and adds a no-data grid plus prepared lighting; these are modifications, not additional measurements.
- Monochrome photograph and labels: New Horizons LORRI `LOR_0299165548_0X630_SCI`, with validation images `LOR_0299165545_0X630_SCI`, `LOR_0299165692_0X630_SCI` and `LOR_0299155705_0X636_SCI`, NASA / Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory / Southwest Research Institute; NASA Planetary Data System. Retain mission, instrument and author attribution. cssEarth displays a stretched, experimental single-observation projection and preserves its acquisition illumination; it does not claim an albedo or color product.
- Encounter orientation: New Horizons PDS SPICE kernel `nh_pcnh_010.tpc`, NASA/JPL NAIF and Southwest Research Institute / New Horizons science team. Retain original attribution and notices. cssEarth derives the camera at the target emission time and fits only two detector translations; this does not establish absolute IAU cartography.
- Scientific context: Weaver et al. (2016), Porter et al. (2019, 2025), Cook et al. (2018), and the PDS New Horizons composition collection. Papers are cited, not redistributed as owned project content.
- Package code and preparation: cssEarth, MIT. Gray areas must remain identified as unobserved or rejected surface coverage.
