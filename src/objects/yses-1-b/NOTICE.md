# YSES 1 b credits

Orbit inputs: Roberts et al. (2025), AJ (VLTI/GRAVITY and literature astrometry), with the CRIRES+ relative radial velocity of Zhang et al. (2024), AJ. Radius, temperature and mass: Bohn et al. (2020), ApJL 898, L16. Disc: Hoch et al. (2025), Nature 643, 938.

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Placement: its star at Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Bohn et al. (2020a), as compiled in Best, Liu, Magnier & Dupuy (2024), The UltracoolSheet v2.1 (Zenodo); zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (2MASS Ks 2.159 µm, 2MASS H 1.662 µm, 2MASS J 1.235 µm).

Limb darkening: Claret, Hauschildt & Witte (2012), A&A 546, A14, CDS J/A+A/546/A14.
