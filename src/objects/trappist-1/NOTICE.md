# TRAPPIST-1 credits

Position, proper motion, parallax and the BP/RP spectrum: ESA/Gaia/DPAC, Gaia Data Release 3 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674,
A1; De Angeli et al. 2023, A&A 674, A2; external calibration Montegriffo et al. 2023, A&A 674, A3). Gaia data are free to use with
acknowledgement.

Sampling of the continuous spectrum: GaiaXPy (Ruz-Mieres 2022, doi:10.5281/zenodo.6674521), the archive's own library.

Radius, mass and the system's parameters: E. Agol and others (2021), The Planetary Science Journal 2, 1, doi:10.3847/PSJ/abd022.

Radial velocity: Jeffers et al. (2020), through SIMBAD (Centre de Données astronomiques de Strasbourg).

Color matching: CIE 1931 2-degree observer, International Commission on Illumination, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Modeled limb darkening: Claret, Hauschildt & Witte (2012), A&A 546, A14, through the CDS (J/A+A/546/A14). The coefficients come
from PHOENIX model atmospheres; no resolved photospheric image is reproduced. Effective temperature and surface gravity: Agol and
others (2021), through the NASA Exoplanet Archive.
