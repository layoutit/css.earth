Radius 0.505 +/- 0.012 solar radii from Castro González et al. 2020, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of K2-324 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020MNRAS.499.5416C/abstract). Mass 0.517 +/- 0.041 solar masses from Castro González et al. 2020, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of K2-324 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020MNRAS.499.5416C/abstract). Temperature 3,752 K from Castro González et al. 2020, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of K2-324 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.74 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** A Planck spectrum at 3,752 K, because no archive holds a spectrum of this star (stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number), through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffcd99. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; planck: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,752 K and log g 4.74 (u1 0.394, u2 0.356): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
