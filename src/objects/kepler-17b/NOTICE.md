# Kepler-17 b credits

Radius: Désert et al. 2011 (2011ApJS..197...14D), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Désert et al. 2011, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2011ApJS..197...14D), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Désert et al. 2011 (2011ApJS..197...14D), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,032 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
