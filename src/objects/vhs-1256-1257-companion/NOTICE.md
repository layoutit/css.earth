# VHS 1256-1257 B credits

Orbit, mass and temperature: Dupuy et al. (2023), MNRAS 519, 1688. Radius: Climent et al. (2022), A&A 660, A65. B is placed by its orbit around VHS 1256-1257 A, whose placement is Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1).

Colour: a Planck spectrum at the published temperature, weighted by the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0). No image of the star's surface exists.
