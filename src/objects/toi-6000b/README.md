# TOI-6000 b

## Sources

It is the only planet known around TOI-6000. Its orbit and size follow Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025's fit, the archive's default. This account was drafted from Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Size and mass.** Radius 0.08386133 Jupiter radii from Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025 (2025AJ....170..148G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025AJ....170..148G/abstract): 5,995.4 km at 71,492 km per Jupiter radius. GM from the mass 0.00245 Jupiter masses (the NASA Exoplanet Archive's calculated value (M-R relationship, its Chen & Kipping 2017 mass-radius relationship): a model, not a measurement, via the NASA Exoplanet Archive, https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/pscp_calc.html) times JPL's Jupiter GM. A sphere: no oblateness is measured.

**Orbit.** Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025 (2025AJ....170..148G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname GOMEZ_BARRIENTOS_ET_AL_2025): P 0.448959 d Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025 (2025AJ....170..148G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname GOMEZ_BARRIENTOS_ET_AL_2025): a/R* 4.66; Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025 (2025AJ....170..148G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname GOMEZ_BARRIENTOS_ET_AL_2025): inclination 87.05 degrees No archive row states an eccentricity; the orbit is taken as circular Gomez Barrientos et al. 2025 (2025AJ....170..148G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname GOMEZ_BARRIENTOS_ET_AL_2025): transit mid-time 2460109.917 BJD, taken as BJD_TDB; with its period, the row that predicts 2026-01-01 best (1 sigma 33 min) Display convention: transit photometry does not measure the orbit's position angle on the sky, so the ascending node is set at position angle 0 (celestial north).

**Colour.** No image or measured colour exists. The neutral gray is lit by toi-6000's measured colour (#ffc988, the colour lens of toi-6000 (src/objects/toi-6000/source/photometry/stellar-color.json)) at the gray's own brightness.
