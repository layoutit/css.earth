Borrelly terrain: NASA/JPL/MICAS, USGS Astrogeology and DLR stereo team. The reviewed PDS DEM tables retain original labels and units. MICAS orthophotography and XYZ cubes come from the rescued mission website; registration is independently verified, while radiometric calibration is not requalified. Preserve these distinctions and source attribution.

Terrain units and their interpretation: Britt et al. (2004), *The morphology and surface processes of Comet 19/P Borrelly*, DOI [10.1016/j.icarus.2003.09.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2003.09.004). The package contains project-authored numerical image measurements and factual captions under MIT, with source attribution. It does not redistribute the paper or its figures.
