## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Dalba et al. 2022. It is also HD 238894. This account was drafted from Dalba et al. 2022's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2151078741786155008, parallax 8.582 ± 0.011 mas (116.52 pc). Radius 1.636 +/- 0.033 solar radii from Dalba et al. 2022, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of TOI-2180 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022AJ....163...61D/abstract). Mass 1.111 +/- 0.047 solar masses from Dalba et al. 2022, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of TOI-2180 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022AJ....163...61D/abstract). Temperature 5,695 K from Dalba et al. 2022, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of TOI-2180 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.06 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2151078741786155008, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #fff3f0. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 238894 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,695 K and log g 4.06 (u1 0.471, u2 0.253): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
