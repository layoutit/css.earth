# HD 110067 d

A sphere with a radius of 2.852 Earth radii, lit by HD 110067. Neutral gray represents unknown surface colour.

## Sources

[Luque et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06692-3), Table 1 and Extended Data Table 3, supply the adopted radius and orbital parameters. The orbit uses the published a/R*, inclination, period and transit epoch. TJD is converted to BMJD_TDB by adding 56999.5. Mass upper limits are not treated as measurements.

## Evidence

The focused hosted-orbit, source, package and rendered-scene checks passed; see the [system evidence](../hd-110067/evidence/README.md). Validation results and browser captures are recorded with the delivery change; no N-body dynamics are claimed.

## Known problems

Circular orbits with the published 2023 periods and transit epochs. Planet-planet perturbations and transit-timing variations are not propagated; this is not a current transit forecast or an N-body resonance simulation. Absolute sky nodes and spin axes are unknown. There is no adopted surface map, atmospheric image or spin model.
