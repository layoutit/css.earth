# CoRoT-2 b credits

Radius: Gillon et al. 2010 (2010A&A...511A...3G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Gillon et al. 2010, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2010A&A...511A...3G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Gillon et al. 2010 (2010A&A...511A...3G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,890 K dayside brightness temperature of Alonso et al. 2010, dayside brightness temperature at 2.15 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
