Its radius and temperature follow Passegger et al. 2024. It is also HIP 115752. This account was drafted from Passegger et al. 2024's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2643842302456085888, parallax 33.725 ± 0.017 mas (29.65 pc). Radius 0.58 +/- 0.03 solar radii from Passegger et al. 2024, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of GJ 9827 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024A&A...684A..22P/abstract). Mass 0.62 +/- 0.04 solar masses from Passegger et al. 2024, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of GJ 9827 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024A&A...684A..22P/abstract). Temperature 4,236 K from Passegger et al. 2024, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of GJ 9827 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.7 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** A Planck spectrum at 4,236 K, because no archive holds a spectrum of this star (stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number), through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffd8b0. **Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 4,236 K and log g 4.7 (u1 0.701, u2 0.082): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
