# K2-266 credits

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 0.703 +/- 0.024 solar radii from Rodriguez et al. 2018, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of K2-266 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2018AJ....156..245R/abstract); Mass 0.686 +/- 0.033 solar masses from Rodriguez et al. 2018, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of K2-266 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2018AJ....156..245R/abstract); temperature from Rodriguez et al. 2018, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of K2-266 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Colour: a Planck spectrum at the temperature of Rodriguez et al. 2018, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of K2-266 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive, through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75.
