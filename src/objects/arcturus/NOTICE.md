# Arcturus credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Lacour, Meimon, Thiébaut et al., "The limb-darkened Arcturus: imaging with the IOTA/IONIC interferometer", A&A 485, 561 (2008), arXiv:0804.0192. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
