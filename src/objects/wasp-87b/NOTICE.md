# WASP-87 b credits

Radius: Addison et al. 2016 (2016ApJ...823...29A), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Addison et al. 2016, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2016ApJ...823...29A), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Addison et al. 2016 (2016ApJ...823...29A), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,956 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 4.5 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
