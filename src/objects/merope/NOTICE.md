# Merope credits

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 8.963 solar radii from McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770, table 2, HIP 17608: radius 7.747 solar radii, implied by the fitted luminosity 211.434 solar luminosities (fractional uncertainty 0.783) and temperature; scaled from the paper's distance, 116.55 pc, to the cluster's, 134.84 pc: at a fixed temperature the radius grows with the distance, so 7.747 x 134.84/116.55 = 8.963 solar radii (luminosity 283.0) (https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stx1433); No mass is measured, so GM is 0, the records' unpublished value; temperature from McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770, table 2, HIP 17608: effective temperature 7913 +/- 3358 K, from the fit of a model atmosphere to the star's spectral energy distribution (goodness of fit Q 0.448).

Color: Kharitonov, Tereshchenko & Knyazeva (1988), Spectrophotometric Catalogue of Stars (Alma-Ata), record 215: HR 1156; VizieR III/202, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75.

Placement: Gaia DR3 source 65205373152172032: position, proper motion; distance: Hunt & Reffert (2023), Improving the open cluster census. II. An all-sky cluster catalogue with Gaia DR3, A&A 673, A114; CDS J/A+A/673/A114, Table 3 (clusters), Melotte_22: dist50 134.84134308 pc, the distance of the Pleiades, of which Merope is a member; its own parallax (McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson 2017 adopt 116.55 pc from Gaia DR1 or the Hipparcos reduction of van Leeuwen 2007) is less certain than the cluster's depth (half its members within 2.70 pc of the centre), and the Hipparcos-era parallaxes of the bright Pleiades are the ones the cluster's distance controversy showed biased.
