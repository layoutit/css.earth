# Merope

## Sources

Its brightness, measured band by band, gives 211 times the Sun's luminosity at 7,913 K, so 7.747 solar radii. It is also HD 23480, HR 1156, HIP 17608. The introduction is generated from McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770's published values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 65205373152172032, distance 117 pc from McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770, table 2, HIP 17608: distance 116.55 pc, the paper's parallax inverted (Gaia DR1's where it revised the star's, else the Hipparcos reduction of van Leeuwen 2007; section 2.3; fractional uncertainty 0.043), at which the luminosity and radius hold; Gaia DR3's parallax, 7.067 ± 0.286 mas (24.7 standard errors), is not used. Radius 7.747 solar radii from McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770, table 2, HIP 17608: radius 7.747 solar radii, implied by the fitted luminosity 211.434 solar luminosities (fractional uncertainty 0.783) and temperature (https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stx1433). No mass is measured, so GM is 0, the records' unpublished value. Temperature 7,913 K from McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770, table 2, HIP 17608: effective temperature 7913 +/- 3358 K, from the fit of a model atmosphere to the star's spectral energy distribution (goodness of fit Q 0.448). No surface gravity of this star is published.

**Color.** Kharitonov, Tereshchenko & Knyazeva (1988), Spectrophotometric Catalogue of Stars (Alma-Ata), record 215: HR 1156; VizieR III/202, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #b1c6ff. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 23480 is not in the library; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: HR 1156 is not in the catalogue; kiehling: HR 1156 is not among its 60 stars; burnashev: BS 1156 is not in part2; kharitonov: used.

**Limb.** No limb darkening is drawn: no mass is measured and no spectroscopic surface gravity is published with this radius: McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770 assume the gravity of their fit (table column 15).
