# M87* credits

Measurements: EHT Collaboration (2019), ApJL 875, L1 (M87 Paper I: distance, mass, angular gravitational radius); EHT
Collaboration (2022), ApJL 930, L17 (Sgr A* Paper VI: the shadow diameter of a non-spinning black hole).

Image: Event Horizon Telescope Collaboration, calibrated data release 2019-D01-01 and imaging pipelines 2019-D01-02
(EHT Collaboration 2019, ApJL 875, L4, M87 Paper IV); reconstructed here.

Placement: SIMBAD, CDS Strasbourg (position of `M 87`, reference 2020A&A...644A.159C).
