# PDS 70 credits

Placement: Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1). This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Cross-identification and spectral type: SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.

Measurements: radius and temperature from Keppler et al. (2018), A&A 617, A44; the star's mass from Trevascus et al. (2025), A&A 698, A19.

Colour: ESO VLT/X-shooter spectra, based on observations collected at the European Organisation for Astronomical Research in the Southern Hemisphere under ESO programme 105.205R.001 (PENELLOPE; Manara et al. 2021, A&A 650, A196), weighted by the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0). Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75. No image of the star's surface exists.

Dust ring (the pds-70-disc volume): ALMA project 2018.A.00030.S (PI M. Benisty), band 7 continuum, member OUS `uid://A001/X13b4/Xb1`, observed 27 July 2019; imaged by the ALMA pipeline and served by the ALMA Science Archive; the observations are published in Benisty et al. (2021, ApJL 916, L2), whose Figure 1 colour map it is shown in. This paper makes use of the following ALMA data: ADS/JAO.ALMA#2018.A.00030.S.
