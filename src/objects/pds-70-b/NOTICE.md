# PDS 70 b credits

Orbit and dynamical mass: Trevascus et al. (2025), A&A 698, A19. Radius and temperature: the atmosphere fits of Wang et al. (2021), AJ 161, 148. No image or colour of the planet's surface exists: it is drawn as a sphere of the model radius in the shared neutral gray.

Placement: the star PDS 70 at its Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).
