# PDS 70 b credits

Orbit and dynamical mass: Trevascus et al. (2025), A&A 698, A19. Radius and temperature: the atmosphere fits of Wang et al. (2021), AJ 161, 148. No image resolves the planet.

Placement: the star PDS 70 at its Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Stolker et al. (2020), A&A 644, A13; zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (NACO L′ 3.77 µm, SPHERE K1 2.102 µm, SPHERE H3 1.666 µm).
