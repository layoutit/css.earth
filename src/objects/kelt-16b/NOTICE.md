# KELT-16 b credits

Radius: Oberst et al. 2017 (2017AJ....153...97O), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Oberst et al. 2017, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2017AJ....153...97O), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Oberst et al. 2017 (2017AJ....153...97O), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 3,273 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 4.5 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
