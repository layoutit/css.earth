# WASP-4 credits

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 0.912 +/- 0.007 solar radii from Baştürk et al. 2025, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of WASP-4 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025MNRAS.541..714B/abstract); Mass 0.899 +/- 0.033 solar masses from Baştürk et al. 2025, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of WASP-4 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025MNRAS.541..714B/abstract); temperature from Bouma et al. 2019, the stellar temperature of WASP-4 b's parameter set from Bouma et al. 2019 (the default leaves it empty) in the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 6535499658122055552, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75.

Placement: Gaia DR3 source 6535499658122055552: position, parallax, proper motion and radial velocity. This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Identifiers: SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.
