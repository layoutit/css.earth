# Spica B credits

Radius: Tkachenko et al. (2016), MNRAS 458, 1964, Table 7: secondary radius 3.74 +/- 0.53 solar radii. Mass: Tkachenko et al. (2016), MNRAS 458, 1964, Table 7: dynamical mass of the secondary 7.21 +/- 0.75 solar masses. Temperature: Tkachenko et al. (2016), MNRAS 458, 1964, Tables 4 and 7: secondary Teff 20,900 +/- 800 K from the disentangled spectrum.

Orbit: Tkachenko et al. (2016), MNRAS 458, 1964, Table 2 (PHOEBE 2.0 solution): P 4.0145 d, T0 HJD 2,454,189.40 the time of periastron passage (Sect. 3; MJD 54188.90, HJD for BJD_TDB), e 0.133, longitude of periastron 255.6 degrees (the primary's, from its radial velocities), i 63.1; a 28.20 solar radii over the primary's 7.47 is 3.775 of its radii. The node's position angle on the sky is not measured and is drawn at 0, a display convention.

Color: a Planck spectrum at the cited temperature through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).
