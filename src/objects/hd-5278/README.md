# HD 5278

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Sozzetti et al. 2021. It is also HIP 3911. This account was drafted from Sozzetti et al. 2021's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 4617759514501503616, parallax 17.418 ± 0.018 mas (57.41 pc). Radius 1.194 +/- 0.017 solar radii from Sozzetti et al. 2021, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of HD 5278 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2021A&A...648A..75S/abstract). Mass 1.126 +/- 0.036 solar masses from Sozzetti et al. 2021, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of HD 5278 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2021A&A...648A..75S/abstract). Temperature 6,203 K from Sozzetti et al. 2021, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of HD 5278 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.34 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 4617759514501503616, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #f7f3ff. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 5278 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 6,203 K and log g 4.34 (u1 0.384, u2 0.302): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Not shown.** HD 5278 c: found without a transit, and no paper's row measures its whole orbit together (period, eccentricity, periastron time and an inclination with an error bar) (found by radial velocity).
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
