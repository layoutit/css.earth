# HD 209458 b credits

Radius, period, transit time and inclination: G. Torres et al., "Improved parameters for extrasolar transiting planets", ApJ 677, 1324 (2008), arXiv:0801.1841. Only published numbers are used; the paper is not redistributed.

Thermal colour: the dayside brightness temperature of Zellem, R. T., Lewis, N. K., Knutson, H. A., et al. (2014), "The 4.5 µm Full-orbit Phase Curve of the Hot Jupiter HD 209458b", ApJ 790, 53, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/790/1/53, as the NASA Exoplanet Archive's emission table lists it.

Temperature map: drawn from the phase-curve fit of the same paper (its Tables 2 and 3). Only the published table values are used; no figure is copied. The map is the inversion of Cowan, N. B. & Agol, E. (2008), ApJ 678, L129, doi:10.1086/589232. This work is based in part on observations made with the Spitzer Space Telescope, operated by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, California Institute of Technology, under a contract with NASA.

Numerical extraction and sphere: cssEarth, MIT.
