Chosen by rule: the bare-rock maximum, no light reflected and no heat carried to the night side; the star's temperature is Murgas et al. 2024, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of Wolf 327 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive, a/R* is Murgas et al. 2024. Nine rocky planets of red dwarfs with a measured day side fall within 13% of this kind of estimate (Coy et al. 2025); GJ 357 b, measured since, is 35% above it; see [the expected glow](../../../docs/color-preparation.md#the-expected-glow-of-a-hot-giant).
