# Procyon B credits

Radius: Bond et al. (2015), ApJ 813, 106, section 8: R_B = 0.01232 +/- 0.00032 solar radii, the radius of Provencal et al. (2002) adjusted to the adopted parallax. Mass: Bond et al. (2015), ApJ 813, 106, Table 10: dynamical mass 0.592 +/- 0.006 solar masses. Temperature: Provencal et al. (2002), ApJ 568, 324, as quoted by Bond et al. (2015), section 8: Teff 7740 +/- 50 K from STIS spectra.

Orbit: Bond et al. (2015), ApJ 813, 106, Table 8: relative visual orbit P 40.84 yr, a 4.3075 arcsec, i 31.408, node 100.683, T0 1968.076 (Besselian year), e 0.39785, omega 89.23 degrees for the companion (the star's is 180 degrees on); a over the adopted parallax 0.285 arcsec is 15.114 au, in units of Procyon A's radius.

Color: a Planck spectrum at the cited temperature through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).
