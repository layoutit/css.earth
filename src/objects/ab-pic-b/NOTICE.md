# AB Pic b credits

Orbit: the posterior of Palma-Bifani et al. (2023), A&A 670, A90, distributed by whereistheplanet (Wang et al. 2021), and their astrometry (Table 1). Radius, temperature and mass: Palma-Bifani et al. (2023).

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Placement: its star at Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Liu Dupuy & Allers (2016); Dupuy & Liu (2012), as compiled in Best, Liu, Magnier & Dupuy (2024), The UltracoolSheet v2.1 (Zenodo); zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (MKO K 2.184 µm, MKO H 1.614 µm, MKO J 1.2417 µm).

Limb darkening: Claret, Hauschildt & Witte (2012), A&A 546, A14, CDS J/A+A/546/A14.
