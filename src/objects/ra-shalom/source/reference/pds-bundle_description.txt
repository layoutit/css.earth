Content description for the Radar shape models of  asteroids compiled by Lawrence bundle
========================================================================================

This data set contains ground-based radar shape models of asteroids generated by the method developed by Hudson (1993) and improved by Magri et al. (2007).  The asteroids were observed from the Arecibo Observatory and the Goldstone facility.  Ostro (1993) provides a summary of the observing systems at each facility.  The table below indicates which observatories were used to estimate the shape models and their respective publications.

2100 Ra-Shalom                Arecibo  Shepard et al. 2008; Shepard et al. 2000
4486 Mithra        Goldstone  Arecibo  Brozovic et al. 2010
4660 Nereus        Goldstone  Arecibo  Brozovic et al. 2009
10115 1992 SK      Goldstone           Busch et al. 2006
29075 1950 DA      Goldstone  Arecibo  Busch et al. 2007
33342 1998 WT24    Goldstone  Arecibo  Busch et al. 2008
54509 YORP         Goldstone  Arecibo  Taylor et al. 2007
66391 1999 KW4     Goldstone  Arecibo  Ostro et al. 2006
136617 1994 CC     Goldstone  Arecibo  Brozovic et al. 2011
276049 2002 CE26              Arecibo  Shepard et al. 2006
341843 2008 EV5    Goldstone  Arecibo  Busch et al. 2011

Nearly all of these observations were monostatic (i.e. transmitting and receiving with the same telescope). A subset of the observations of  asteroid (33342) 1998 WT24 used Goldstone's DSS-14 to transmit and DSS-13 to receive (i.e. bistatic). 

The polyhedral shape models are specified by the coordinates of the vertices of the polyhedron in conjunction with the linkages of the vertices into triangular facets. The origin of the models is the center of mass and the axes are the principal axes. The z-axis is the spin axis or positive pole. Units are in kilometers. The shape model files are equivalent to wavefront or .obj formats. The models have the following format:

v x1 y1 z1
v x2 y2 z2
v x3 y3 z3
(etc.)
f i1 j1 k1
f i2 j2 k2
f i3 j3 k3
(etc.)

where the letter 'v' denotes a vertex and the letter 'f' denotes a triangular facet. The vertex table is the first part of the files and describes the positions of the vertices of the shape model. The floating point numbers x1 y1 z1 are the coordinates of vertex 1 etc. The linking of the vertices into facets is provided in the facet table and listed in the second half of the files.  The units of the vertices and facets are kilometers (km).

Files containing the target's spin state include the pole's ecliptic latitude and longitude with their uncertainties, in degrees; the rotational period and uncertainty, in hours; the zero-time epoch with the target's rotation phase, in degrees, at the initial epoch.

Each shape model is presented in a peer review journal referenced below. Users are encouraged to consult the published paper for caveats and uncertainties in the data.
