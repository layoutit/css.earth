# WASP-103 b credits

Atmospheric temperature: Kreidberg, L., Line, M. R., Parmentier, V., et al. (2018), "Global Climate and Atmospheric Composition of the Ultra-hot Jupiter WASP-103b from HST and Spitzer Phase Curve Observations", AJ 156, 17, [doi:10.3847/1538-3881/aac3df](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/aac3df). SPARC/MITgcm output supplied by Vivien Parmentier and publicly released in [the author repository](https://github.com/lkreidberg/2017_wasp103/tree/425f2e8fe694760266398257a71eaa991ab322fd/Figures/GCM_From_Vivien). The repository states no explicit data licence. cssEarth acquires its numerical results and prepares attributed false-colour visualizations; no paper figure or upstream implementation is redistributed.

Radius: Gillon et al. 2014 (2014A&A...562L...3G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Gillon et al. 2014, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2014A&A...562L...3G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Gillon et al. 2014 (2014A&A...562L...3G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.
