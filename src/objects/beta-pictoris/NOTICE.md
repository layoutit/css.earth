# Beta Pictoris credits

Shape: a sphere of the VLTI/VINCI limb-darkened angular diameter (Di Folco et al. 2004, A&A 426, 601) at the Gaia DR3 distance. No image of the photosphere is shown.

Colour: the Gaia DR3 BP/RP sampled spectrum of source 4792774797545800832 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1; De Angeli et al. 2023, A&A 674, A2; Montegriffo et al. 2023, A&A 674, A3) through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b). This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC).

Limb darkening: A. Claret and S. Bloemen, "Gravity and limb-darkening coefficients for the Kepler, CoRoT, Spitzer, uvby, UBVRIJHK, and Sloan photometric systems", A&A 529, A75 (2011), doi:10.1051/0004-6361/201116451, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75.

Placement: Gaia DR3 position, parallax, proper motion and radial velocity. Mass: Lacour et al. (2021), A&A 654, L2.

Debris disc: see the attached volume beta-pictoris-disc (JWST/NIRCam programme 4758, PI Y. Zhou; Zhou et al. 2026).
