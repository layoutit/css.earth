# TOI-622 b

## Sources

It is the only planet known around TOI-622. Its orbit and size follow Psaridi et al. 2023's fit, the archive's default. The introduction is generated from Psaridi et al. 2023's published values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Size and mass.** Radius 0.824 Jupiter radii from Psaridi et al. 2023 (2023A&A...675A..39P), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023A&A...675A..39P/abstract): 58,909.4 km at 71,492 km per Jupiter radius. GM from the mass 0.303 Jupiter masses (Psaridi et al. 2023, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2023A&A...675A..39P), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive, https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023A&A...675A..39P/abstract) times JPL's Jupiter GM. A sphere: no oblateness is measured.

**Orbit.** ExoFOP, via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname EXOFOP): P 6.40252947289 d Psaridi et al. 2023 (2023A&A...675A..39P), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname PSARIDI_ET_AL__2023): a/R* 10.637; Psaridi et al. 2023 (2023A&A...675A..39P), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname PSARIDI_ET_AL__2023): inclination 86.62 degrees No archive row states an eccentricity; the orbit is taken as circular ExoFOP, via the NASA Exoplanet Archive ps table (pl_refname EXOFOP): transit mid-time 2460006.078147 BJD, taken as BJD_TDB; with its period, the row that predicts 2026-01-01 best (1 sigma 1 min) Display convention: transit photometry does not measure the orbit's position angle on the sky, so the ascending node is set at position angle 0 (celestial north).

**Color.** An estimate, not a measurement: a black body at the 1,388 K equilibrium temperature of Psaridi et al. 2023, equilibrium temperature (NASA Exoplanet Archive planetary systems table): #ff6300. Chosen by rule: 1 paper in the archive prints one; the archive's default parameter set. On 120 hot Jupiters whose day side Spitzer measured, the measured temperature is within 20% of this kind of estimate for 83% (Deming et al. 2023); see [the expected glow](../../../docs/color-preparation.md#the-expected-glow-of-a-hot-giant). Reflected starlight is not included.

**Charts.** The orbits of TOI-622's planets from above, from their hosted-orbit records, and its transit in 3 TESS sectors (62, 88, 89), folded onto its orbit. Upper limits and rows without an error are left out.
