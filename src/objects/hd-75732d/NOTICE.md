# 55 Cnc d credits

Radius: the NASA Exoplanet Archive's calculated radius (its Chen & Kipping 2017 mass-radius relationship): a model, not a measurement, since 55 Cnc d does not transit. Mass: Xiao et al. (2026), Table 4: 3.23 (+0.29, -0.30) Jupiter masses, the true mass from radial velocities joined with Hipparcos and Gaia astrometry.

Orbit: Xiao et al. (2026), Table 4, a joint fit of radial velocities with Hipparcos and Gaia astrometry (the time of conjunction alone is Moutou et al. 2026, A&A 705, A190, because Xiao et al. print no epoch; a/R* is their 5.58 au over the star's recorded 656,045 km).
