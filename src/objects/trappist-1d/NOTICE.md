# TRAPPIST-1d credits

Radius, mass, density, orbital period, semi-major axis and insolation: E. Agol, C. Dorn, S. L. Grimm and others (2021),
"Refining the Transit-timing and Photometric Analysis of TRAPPIST-1", The Planetary Science Journal 2, 1, doi:10.3847/PSJ/abd022.
Stellar mass from Mann et al. (2019), as that paper adopts.

Placement: the host star's Gaia DR3 astrometry, with this planet's transit-fitted orbit around it.

The sphere's neutral gray is a display convention of this project, not a measurement.

Illustration lens: NASA's artist's concept map of TRAPPIST-1d from Eyes on Exoplanets, NASA/JPL-Caltech (https://eyes.nasa.gov/apps/exo/assets/image/exoplanet/TRAPPIST-1_d.jpg), used unchanged under NASA's media guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). An artist's concept, not an observation.
