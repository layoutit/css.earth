# KELT-1 b credits

Radius: Siverd et al. 2012 (2012ApJ...761..123S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Siverd et al. 2012, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2012ApJ...761..123S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Siverd et al. 2012 (2012ApJ...761..123S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,943 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
