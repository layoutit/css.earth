# GJ 341

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow DiTomasso et al. 2025. It is also HD 304636, HIP 45908. This account was drafted from DiTomasso et al. 2025's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 5299440441521812992, parallax 95.698 ± 0.015 mas (10.45 pc). Radius 0.5066 +/- 0.0169 solar radii from DiTomasso et al. 2025, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of GJ 341 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025ApJ...979..214D/abstract). Mass 0.48 +/- 0.03 solar masses from DiTomasso et al. 2025, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of GJ 341 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025ApJ...979..214D/abstract). Temperature 3,770 K from DiTomasso et al. 2025, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of GJ 341 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.71 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 5299440441521812992, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffbf88. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 304636 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,770 K and log g 4.71 (u1 0.410, u2 0.342): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
