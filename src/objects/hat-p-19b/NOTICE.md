# HAT-P-19 b credits

Radius: Kang et al. 2024 (2024MNRAS.528.1930K), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Kang et al. 2024, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2024MNRAS.528.1930K), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Kang et al. 2024 (2024MNRAS.528.1930K), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,090 K dayside brightness temperature of Kammer et al. 2015, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
