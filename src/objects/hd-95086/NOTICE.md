# HD 95086 credits

Placement: Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1). This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Temperature and mass: Desgrange et al. (2022), A&A 664, A139, Table 1. Radius: Gaia DR3 FLAME (Creevey et al. 2023). Radial velocity: Gaia DR3.

Color: Gaia DR3 BP/RP sampled spectrum (De Angeli et al. 2023, A&A 674, A2; Montegriffo et al. 2023, A&A 674, A3), weighted by the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0). No image of the star's surface exists.

Dust ring (the hd-95086-disc volume): ALMA project 2013.1.00612.S (PI M. Booth), band 6 continuum, member OUS `uid://A001/X145/X232`, observed between 10 April and 2 May 2015; imaged by the ARI-L project (Massardi et al. 2021, PASP 133, 085001) and served by the ALMA Science Archive; the observations are published in Su et al. (2017, AJ 154, 225), whose beam and Figure 3 color scale it is shown in, whose point sources are removed from it and whose ring fit gives it depth. This paper makes use of the following ALMA data: ADS/JAO.ALMA#2013.1.00612.S.
