**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 3578046995560843520, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #fff1ec. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,858 K and log g 4.43 (u1 0.443, u2 0.270): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
