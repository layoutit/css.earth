The observation lenses are source measurements prepared into declared display
palettes. The normal lens preserves OPAL weather detail with a prepare-time
colour calibration against the cited true-colour reconstruction. The retained
PolyCSS surface uses a declared, source-derived northern coverage treatment; no
weather detail is invented.
The lenses are not live telescope feeds. Orbital motion and apparent moon sizes
are presentation-scaled while identities, ordering, and cited physical facts
remain source-bound.
