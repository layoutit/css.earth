# Fomalhaut credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Mamajek, "On the Age and Binarity of Fomalhaut", ApJ 754, L20 (2012), arXiv:1206.6353. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.

Dust ring (the fomalhaut-disc volume): ALMA project 2015.1.00966.S (PI P. Kalas), band 6 continuum, member OUS `uid://A001/X2d8/X7c`, observed on 29 and 30 December 2015 and 14 January 2016; imaged by the ARI-L project (Massardi et al. 2021, PASP 133, 085001) and served by the ALMA Science Archive; the observations are published in MacGregor et al. (2017, ApJ 842, 8), whose Figure 1 beam and color scale it is shown in, and whose point-source fluxes it uses. The near side is from Kalas et al. (2013, ApJ 775, 56). This paper makes use of the following ALMA data: ADS/JAO.ALMA#2015.1.00966.S.
