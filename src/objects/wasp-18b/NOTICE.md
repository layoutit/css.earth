# WASP-18b credits

Maps: the Eigenspectra products of Challener, R. C., Weiner Mansfield, M., Cubillos, P. E., Piette, A. A. A., et al. (2025), "Horizontal and vertical exoplanet thermal structure from a JWST spectroscopic eclipse map", Nature Astronomy, doi:10.1038/s41550-025-02666-9, from their Zenodo deposit doi:10.5281/zenodo.14751570 (CC BY 4.0), read unchanged. The Eigenspectra method is Mansfield et al. (2020); the code is github.com/meganmansfield/eigenspectra (MIT). The data are from the JWST Transiting Exoplanet Community Early Release Science program 1366 (NASA/ESA/CSA JWST, STScI MAST).

Orbit, radius and mass: the deposited Eigenspectra fit and ThERESA configuration; Coulombe, L.-P., et al. (2023), "A broadband thermal emission spectrum of the ultra-hot Jupiter WASP-18b", Nature 620, 292, doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06230-1.

Temperature–pressure profiles: original HyDRA and Pyrat Bay hotspot tables from Challener et al. (2025), [Zenodo 14751570](https://zenodo.org/records/14751570), and the HyDRA whole-dayside table from Coulombe et al. (2023), [Zenodo 7907569](https://zenodo.org/records/7907569), both CC BY 4.0. The tables are preserved unchanged. The chart converts pressure units, selects the displayed pressure range and plots the published medians and 1σ intervals; see the README for processing and limits.
