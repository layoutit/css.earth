# Kepler-438

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Torres et al. 2015. This account was drafted from Torres et al. 2015's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2104675781979819776, parallax 5.559 ± 0.098 mas (179.88 pc); its RUWE is 6.0, so the single-star astrometry fits poorly, and the parallax is used as published. Radius 0.52 +/- 0.038 solar radii from Torres et al. 2015, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of Kepler-438 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015ApJ...800...99T/abstract). Mass 0.544 +/- 0.041 solar masses from Torres et al. 2015, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of Kepler-438 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015ApJ...800...99T/abstract). Temperature 3,748 K from Torres et al. 2015, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of Kepler-438 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.74 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2104675781979819776, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc18c. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,748 K and log g 4.74 (u1 0.393, u2 0.357): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person; their quotes are sentences of the Wikipedia article "Kepler-438" (revision 1330343691), verbatim, CC BY-SA 4.0.
