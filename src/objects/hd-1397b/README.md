# HD 1397 b

## Sources

It is the only planet known around HD 1397. Its orbit and size follow Nielsen et al. 2019's fit, the archive's default. This account was drafted from Nielsen et al. 2019's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Size and mass.** Radius 1.026 Jupiter radii from Nielsen et al. 2019 (2019A&A...623A.100N), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019A&A...623A.100N/abstract): 73,350.8 km at 71,492 km per Jupiter radius. GM from the mass 0.415 Jupiter masses (Nielsen et al. 2019, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2019A&A...623A.100N), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive, https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019A&A...623A.100N/abstract) times JPL's Jupiter GM. A sphere: no oblateness is measured.

## Known problems

- **Orbit convention.** omega 2.1 degrees is taken as Nielsen et al. 2019 gives it through the archive (pl_orblper); papers differ on whether that is the star's or the planet's argument of periastron. The epoch is the transit, so a swapped convention would only mirror the ellipse (e 0.251) about the line of sight.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
