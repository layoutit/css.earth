# DH Tau b credits

Orbit inputs: Bowler et al. (2020), AJ, doi:10.3847/1538-3881/ab5b11, with the literature astrometry they compile. Radius, mass and temperature: Xuan et al. (2024), evolutionary models.

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Placement: its star at Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Itoh et al. (2005), as compiled in Best, Liu, Magnier & Dupuy (2024), The UltracoolSheet v2.1 (Zenodo); zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (MKO K 2.184 µm, MKO H 1.614 µm, MKO J 1.2417 µm).

Limb darkening: Claret, Hauschildt & Witte (2012), A&A 546, A14, CDS J/A+A/546/A14.
