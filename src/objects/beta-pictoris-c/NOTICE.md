# Beta Pictoris c credits

Shape: a sphere of the model radius (Nowak et al. 2020); no image of the planet exists. The gray is a display convention.

Orbit and mass: Lacour et al. (2021), A&A 654, L2, GRAVITY astrometry and HARPS radial velocities in the orbitize! conventions of Blunt et al. (2020).

Rotation: none measured; the display axis is the orbit normal.

Placement: the star Beta Pictoris at its Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).
