# WASP-131 b credits

Radius: Doyle et al. 2023 (2023MNRAS.522.4499D), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Doyle et al. 2023, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2023MNRAS.522.4499D), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Doyle et al. 2023 (2023MNRAS.522.4499D), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,418 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
