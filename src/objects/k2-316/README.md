Its radius and temperature follow Castro González et al. 2020. This account was drafted from Castro González et al. 2020's values; the sections below are the data's own.

Radius 0.379 +/- 0.003 solar radii from Castro González et al. 2020, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of K2-316 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020MNRAS.499.5416C/abstract). Mass 0.408 +/- 0.005 solar masses from Castro González et al. 2020, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of K2-316 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020MNRAS.499.5416C/abstract). Temperature 3,436 K from Castro González et al. 2020, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of K2-316 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.89 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** A Planck spectrum at 3,436 K, because no archive holds a spectrum of this star (stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number), through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc588. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; planck: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret (2017), A&A 600, A30 computes from PHOENIX model atmospheres for the TESS band at 3,436 K and log g 4.89 (u1 0.164, u2 0.439): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
