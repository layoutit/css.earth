# PDS 70 c credits

Orbit and dynamical mass: Trevascus et al. (2025), A&A 698, A19. Radius and temperature: the atmosphere fits of Wang et al. (2021), AJ 161, 148. No image or color of the planet's surface exists: it is drawn as a sphere of the model radius in the shared neutral gray.

Placement: the star PDS 70 at its Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Limb darkening: computed with PICASO 4.1 (Batalha et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 70) on Exo-REM cloudy (Charnay et al. 2018, ApJ 854, 172; 1 times solar metallicity, C/O 0.50) profiles (https://lesia.obspm.fr/exorem/YGP_grids/old_grids_2021/) and PICASO's resampled opacity database (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3759675). Model fit: Wang et al. (2021), AJ 161, 148.
