## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Adams et al. 2021. This account was drafted from Adams et al. 2021's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2538051863476537984, parallax 5.612 ± 0.019 mas (178.19 pc). Radius 0.89 +/- 0.15 solar radii from Adams et al. 2021, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of K2-210 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2021PSJ.....2..152A/abstract). Mass 0.911717 +/- 0.02297 solar masses from Mayo et al. 2018, the stellar mass of K2-210 b's parameter set from Mayo et al. 2018 (the default leaves it empty) in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2018AJ....155..136M/abstract). Temperature 5,500 K from Adams et al. 2021, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of K2-210 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.5 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2538051863476537984, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffeadd. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,500 K and log g 4.5 (u1 0.521, u2 0.220): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
