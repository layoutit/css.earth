**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2089570519439964416, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffe3d2. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 235088 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,075 K and log g 4.59 (u1 0.636, u2 0.136): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
