# Kepler-41 b credits

Radius: Bonomo et al. 2015 (2015A&A...575A..85B), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Bonomo et al. 2015, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2015A&A...575A..85B), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Bonomo et al. 2015 (2015A&A...575A..85B), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,400 K dayside brightness temperature of Esteves et al. 2015, dayside brightness temperature in the Kepler band, assuming no reflected light, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
