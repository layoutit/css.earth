# GQ Lup b credits

Orbit inputs: Venkatesan et al. (2025), ApJ 993, 69 (VLTI/GRAVITY astrometry and orbit fit), with the literature astrometry and CRIRES radial velocity they compile. Radius and temperature: Stolker et al. (2021), AJ. Mass: Xuan et al. (2024).

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Placement: its star at Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Marois et al. (2007); Stolker et al. (2021); McElwain et al. (2007), as compiled in Best, Liu, Magnier & Dupuy (2024), The UltracoolSheet v2.1 (Zenodo); zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (MKO K 2.184 µm, MKO H 1.614 µm, 2MASS J 1.235 µm).

Limb darkening: Claret, Hauschildt & Witte (2012), A&A 546, A14, CDS J/A+A/546/A14.
