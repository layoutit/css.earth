# HD 238108

## Sources

M40, also called Winnecke 4, is an optical double: this star lies nearer than its apparent partner, HD 238107. The introduction is generated from Merrifield, Gray & Haran (2016)'s published values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 1575047731026006528, parallax 6.933 ± 0.016 mas (144.24 pc). Radius 1.107 (1.085 to 1.130) from Gaia DR3 FLAME (Creevey et al. 2023, A&A 674, A26; astrophysical_parameters of the same source). Mass 1.124 (1.084 to 1.164) from Gaia DR3 FLAME (Creevey et al. 2023, A&A 674, A26; astrophysical_parameters of the same source). Temperature 6,095.4 K from Gaia DR3 astrophysical parameters (CDS I/355/paramp), source 1575047731026006528: Teff 6095.4 K from the BP/RP spectra (GSP-Phot). log g 4.4 from the mass and radius.

**Color.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 1575047731026006528, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #f8f3ff. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 238108 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 6,095.4 K and log g 4.4 (u1 0.400, u2 0.294): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Not shown.** M40 has no package of its own: it is two unrelated stars, each drawn as its own body.
