# Iota Persei credits

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 1.412 +/- 0.009 solar radii from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 746, 101, HD 19373: radius in solar radii, from the limb-darkened angular diameter 1.246 +/- 0.008 mas (CHARA) and the Hipparcos parallax, 1.412 +/- 0.009 (https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/746/1/101); Mass 1.169 +/- 0.013 solar masses from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 746, 101, HD 19373: mass in solar masses, from Yonsei-Yale isochrones at the measured radius and temperature (a model value), 1.169 +/- 0.013 (https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/746/1/101); temperature from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 746, 101, HD 19373: effective temperature in K, from the angular diameter and the bolometric flux, 5915 +/- 29.

Colour: Pulkovo spectrophotometric catalogue, table 5 (320-1080 nm, 2.5 nm steps, 10 nm resolution, absolute flux in W m^-2 m^-1): Alekseeva et al. (1996, 1997), Baltic Astronomy 5, 603 and 6, 481; VizieR III/201. Iota Persei is HR 937., through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b). Cross-check: Kharitonov, Tereshchenko & Knyazeva (1988), Spectrophotometric Catalogue of Stars (Alma-Ata), record 162: HR 937; VizieR III/202.

Limb darkening: Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75, via VizieR J/A+A/529/A75.

Placement: Gaia DR3 source 436648129327098496: position, proper motion and radial velocity; distance: Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 746, 101, HD 19373: the Hipparcos parallax the radius was computed with (van Leeuwen 2007), 94.87 +/- 0.23 mas, inverted. This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Identifiers: SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.
