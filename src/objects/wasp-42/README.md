Its radius and temperature follow Southworth et al. 2016. This account was drafted from Southworth et al. 2016's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 6139698733664726784, parallax 5.621 ± 0.015 mas (177.91 pc). Radius 0.892 +/- 0.026 solar radii from Southworth et al. 2016, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of WASP-42 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2016MNRAS.457.4205S/abstract). Mass 0.951 +/- 0.063 solar masses from Southworth et al. 2016, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of WASP-42 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2016MNRAS.457.4205S/abstract). Temperature 5,315 K from Southworth et al. 2016, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of WASP-42 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.52 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 6139698733664726784, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffdec5. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 5,315 K and log g 4.52 (u1 0.569, u2 0.186): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person; their quotes are sentences of the Wikipedia article "WASP-42" (revision 1374437584), verbatim, CC BY-SA 4.0.
