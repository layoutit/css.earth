# HAT-P-13 b credits

Radius: Winn et al. 2010 (2010ApJ...718..575W), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Winn et al. 2010, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2010ApJ...718..575W), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Winn et al. 2010 (2010ApJ...718..575W), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,732 K dayside brightness temperature of Hardy et al. 2017, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
