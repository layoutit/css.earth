# Polaris credits

Shape: a sphere of the limb-darkened diameter measured by Evans, Schaefer, Gallenne et al., "The Orbit and Dynamical Mass of Polaris: Observations with the CHARA Array", ApJ 971, 190 (2024), arXiv:2407.09641. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position and proper motion from van Leeuwen, A&A 474, 653 (2007); radial velocity from the SB9 catalogue, Pourbaix et al., A&A 424, 727 (2004); distance and mass for the display GM from Evans et al. (2024). No measured rotation axis: the display axis is celestial north.
