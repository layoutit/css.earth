# π¹ Gruis credits

Photograph: reconstructed in this repository with SQUEEZE 3.0 (Fabien Baron, Georgia State University; GPL-3.0; commit 4d34e877) from the calibrated VLTI/PIONIER interferometric data of September 2014 published by Claudia Paladini in the JMMC Optical interferometry DataBase (OiDB). The observations and their published reconstruction are Paladini, Baron, Jorissen et al., "Large granulation cells on the surface of the giant star π¹ Gruis", Nature 553, 310 (2018), doi:10.1038/nature25001. Acknowledge ESO/VLTI/PIONIER and the paper when reusing the image.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion and parallax from Gaia DR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2020). Mass for the display GM: Mayer et al., A&A 570, A113 (2014). No measured rotation axis: the display axis is celestial north.
