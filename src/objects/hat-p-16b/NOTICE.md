# HAT-P-16 b credits

Radius: Alexoudi 2022 (2022AN....34324012A), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Bonomo et al. 2017, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2017A&A...602A.107B), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Alexoudi 2022 (2022AN....34324012A), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,855 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 4.5 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
