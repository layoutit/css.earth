# Proxima Centauri credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Demory, Ségransan, Forveille et al., "Mass-radius relation of low and very low-mass stars revisited with the VLTI", A&A 505, 205 (2009), arXiv:0906.0602. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
