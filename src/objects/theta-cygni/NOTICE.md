# Theta Cygni credits

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 1.697 +/- 0.03 solar radii from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 746, 101, HD 185395: radius in solar radii, from the limb-darkened angular diameter 0.861 +/- 0.015 mas (CHARA) and the Hipparcos parallax, 1.697 +/- 0.03 (https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/746/1/101); Mass 1.342 +/- 0.011 solar masses from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 746, 101, HD 185395: mass in solar masses, from Yonsei-Yale isochrones at the measured radius and temperature (a model value), 1.342 +/- 0.011 (https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/746/1/101); temperature from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 746, 101, HD 185395: effective temperature in K, from the angular diameter and the bolometric flux, 6381 +/- 65.

Color: Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 2135110401277424384, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).
