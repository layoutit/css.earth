# Venera surface-image rights and provenance

The four checked image files are exact downloads from the NASA Planetary Data
System Geosciences Node Venera page. That page says the files are provided by
courtesy of the Vernadsky Institute and Moscow Power Institute and that the
Venera data are not formally archived in PDS. The repository therefore credits
those institutions and describes NASA PDS as the distributor, not the creator.

The files are original automatic probe-camera records in the institutional
archive's historical assembly and tonal processing. The repository treats the
camera records as public-domain scientific data because they were recorded
autonomously without a human photographer choosing the captured moment or
composition. Section 80 of Russian Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 10 of
23 April 2019 excludes results produced by technical means without human
creative activity from copyright. The United States Copyright Office likewise
requires human authorship for copyright protection. Wikimedia Commons applies
the Russian automatic-camera rule to Venera imagery.

This qualification does not cover later colorizations, gap filling,
geometrical corrections, AI enhancement, or other creative restorations. No
such restoration is used here.
