# 55 Cnc e credits

Radius: Bourrier et al. 2018 (2018A&A...619A...1B), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Bourrier et al. 2018, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2018A&A...619A...1B), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Bourrier et al. 2018 (2018A&A...619A...1B), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Colour: a black body at the 2,360 K dayside brightness temperature of Demory et al. 2012, dayside brightness temperature at 4.5 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions.

Illustration lens: base-colour texture of the 55 Cnc e 3D Model by NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD), https://science.nasa.gov/resource/55-cancri-e-3d-model/, used unchanged under NASA's media guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). An artist's illustration, not an observation.
