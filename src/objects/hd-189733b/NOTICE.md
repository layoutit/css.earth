# HD 189733b credits

Temperature map: M. Lally, R. C. Challener, N. K. Lewis, J. Inglis et al., "Eclipse Mapping with MIRI: 2D Map of HD 189733b from 8μm JWST MIRI LRS Observations", arXiv:2503.20895 (2025); data deposit "Data Products for Eclipse Mapping with MIRI: 2D Map of HD 189733b from 8μm JWST MIRI LRS Observations", Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.15103479, CC BY 4.0, used unchanged except that longitudes the observations never faced are not drawn, as in the authors' own figures. Based on observations made with the NASA/ESA/CSA James Webb Space Telescope, MIRI/LRS program GO 2021 (PI B. Kilpatrick), and with the Spitzer Space Telescope. The colours are false colour chosen here (the plasma palette); the values are the authors'.

Radius and orbit: the deposited ThERESA configuration (Lally et al. 2025, Table 1). Tidal locking is assumed, as in that paper.

Temperature uncertainty: `tmap_unc` in the same Lally et al. (2025) Zenodo deposit, CC BY 4.0. Prepared with the same observed-longitude mask as the temperature map and a project-selected viridis false-colour scale. Values are the authors' standard deviations within the fitted model.

Reflected-light chart: factual numerical transcription of the six-channel HST/STIS geometric albedos, band edges, central wavelengths and uncertainties in Thomas M. Evans et al. (2013), Table 1, “The Deep Blue Color of HD 189733b: Albedo Measurements with HST/STIS at Visible Wavelengths”, doi:10.1088/2041-8205/772/2/L16 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.3239). Based on NASA/ESA Hubble Space Telescope observations, program GO-13006. No paper figures are reproduced.

Broadband-albedo chart: factual numerical transcription of the CHEOPS result in A. F. Krenn et al. (2023), “The geometric albedo of the hot Jupiter HD 189733b measured with CHEOPS”, doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202245016 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.07731). CHEOPS is an ESA mission with Switzerland and a European consortium. No paper figures are reproduced.

Transmission-spectrum chart: Guangwei Fu et al. (2024), “Hydrogen sulfide and metal-enriched atmosphere for a Jupiter-mass exoplanet”, doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07760-y. JWST NIRCam data and free-retrieval median model from Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.11459715, version 1 (https://zenodo.org/records/11459715), CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The two archive members retain their original bytes. The prepared chart converts depth and error fractions to percentages and restricts the displayed model to the measured wavelength region.

Illustration lens: NASA's artist's concept map of HD 189733b from Eyes on Exoplanets, NASA/JPL-Caltech (https://eyes.nasa.gov/apps/exo/assets/image/exoplanet/HD_189733_b.jpg), used unchanged under NASA's media guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). An artist's concept, not an observation.
