# Barnard's Star

## Sources

Interferometry gives it 0.1869 solar radii; with its total light, that makes its surface 3,222 K. It is also HIP 87937. The introduction is generated from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 757, 112's published values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 4472832130942575872, parallax 546.976 ± 0.040 mas (1.83 pc). Radius 0.1869 +/- 0.0012 solar radii from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 757, 112, Table 6, GJ 699: radius in solar radii, 0.1869 +/- 0.0012, from the weighted mean of the interferometric measurements the table lists (https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/757/2/112). Mass 0.146 solar masses from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 757, 112, Table 6, GJ 699: mass 0.146 solar masses from the K-band mass-luminosity relation of Henry & McCarthy (1993), not a dynamical mass (https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/757/2/112). Temperature 3,222 K from Boyajian et al. (2012), ApJ 757, 112, Table 6, GJ 699: effective temperature in K, 3222 +/- 10, from the weighted mean of the interferometric measurements the table lists. log g 5.06 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 4472832130942575872, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc77a. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret (2017), A&A 600, A30 computes from PHOENIX model atmospheres for the TESS band at 3,222 K and log g 5.06 (u1 0.153, u2 0.475): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
