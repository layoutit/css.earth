# WASP-36 b credits

Radius: Mancini et al. 2016 (2016MNRAS.459.1393M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Mancini et al. 2016, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2016MNRAS.459.1393M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Mancini et al. 2016 (2016MNRAS.459.1393M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,900 K dayside brightness temperature of Zhou et al. 2015, dayside brightness temperature at 2.159 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
