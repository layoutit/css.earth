# HIP 65 A

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Nielsen et al. 2020. It is also HIP 65. This account was drafted from Nielsen et al. 2020's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 4923860051276772608, parallax 16.164 ± 0.009 mas (61.87 pc). Radius 0.7242 +/- 0.0081 solar radii from Nielsen et al. 2020, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of HIP 65 A b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020A&A...639A..76N/abstract). Mass 0.781 +/- 0.027 solar masses from Nielsen et al. 2020, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of HIP 65 A b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020A&A...639A..76N/abstract). Temperature 4,590 K from Nielsen et al. 2020, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of HIP 65 A b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.61 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 4923860051276772608, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffcdac. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 4,590 K and log g 4.61 (u1 0.759, u2 0.034): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
