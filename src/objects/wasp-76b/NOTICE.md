# WASP-76b credits

Temperature map: the SPIDERMAN spherical-harmonic fit of May, E. M., Komacek, T. D., Stevenson, K. B., et al. (2021), "Spitzer Phase-curve Observations and Circulation Models of the Inflated Ultrahot Jupiter WASP-76b", AJ 162, 158, doi:10.3847/1538-3881/ac0e30, evaluated with SPIDERMAN (Louden, T. & Kreidberg, L. 2018, MNRAS 477, 2613, doi:10.1093/mnras/sty558). Only the published table values are used; no figure is copied. This work is based in part on observations made with the Spitzer Space Telescope, operated by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, California Institute of Technology, under a contract with NASA.

Radius, mass and orbit: Ehrenreich, D., et al. (2020), "Nightside condensation of iron in an ultrahot giant exoplanet", Nature 580, 597, doi:10.1038/s41586-020-2107-1.
