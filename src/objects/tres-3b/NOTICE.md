# TrES-3 b credits

Radius: Sozzetti et al. 2009 (2009ApJ...691.1145S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Sozzetti et al. 2009, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2009ApJ...691.1145S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Sozzetti et al. 2009 (2009ApJ...691.1145S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,731 K dayside brightness temperature of Croll et al. 2010, dayside brightness temperature at 2.146 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
