## Known problems

- **Orbit convention.** omega 155.5 degrees is taken as Zhang et al. 2024 gives it through the archive (pl_orblper); papers differ on whether that is the star's or the planet's argument of periastron. The epoch is the transit, so a swapped convention would only mirror the ellipse (e 0.3136) about the line of sight.
