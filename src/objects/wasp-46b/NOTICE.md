# WASP-46 b credits

Radius: Ciceri et al. 2016 (2016MNRAS.456..990C), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Ciceri et al. 2016, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2016MNRAS.456..990C), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Ciceri et al. 2016 (2016MNRAS.456..990C), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,200 K dayside brightness temperature of Zhou et al. 2015, dayside brightness temperature at 2.159 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
