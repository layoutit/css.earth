# KELT-9b credits

Temperature map: drawn from the Spitzer phase-curve fit of Mansfield, M., Bean, J. L., Stevenson, K. B., et al. (2020), "Evidence for H2 Dissociation and Recombination Heat Transport in the Atmosphere of KELT-9b", ApJL 888, L15, doi:10.3847/2041-8213/ab5b09. Only the published table values are used; no figure is copied. The map is the inversion of Cowan, N. B. & Agol, E. (2008), ApJ 678, L129, doi:10.1086/589232. This work is based in part on observations made with the Spitzer Space Telescope, operated by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, California Institute of Technology, under a contract with NASA.

Orbit, mass and transit time: Gaudi, B. S., et al. (2017), Nature 546, 514, doi:10.1038/nature22392. Radius: Ahlers, J. P., et al. (2020), AJ 160, 4, doi:10.3847/1538-3881/ab8fa3.
