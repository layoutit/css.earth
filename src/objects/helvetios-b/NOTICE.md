# 51 Peg b credits

Radius: the NASA Exoplanet Archive's calculated radius (its Chen & Kipping 2017 mass-radius relationship): a model, not a measurement, since 51 Peg b does not transit. Mass: Cont et al. (2026), A&A 710, A345, Table 1: 0.61 (+0.06, -0.05) Jupiter masses, from the Doppler shift of the planet's own spectral lines.

Orbit: Cont et al. (2026), A&A 710, A345, Tables 1 and 2 (tilt 49.8 +5.8/-5.7 degrees from the planet's own Doppler shift; eccentricity fixed to zero, 2-sigma limit 0.0063; a/R* is its 0.052 au over the star's recorded 847,269 km).
