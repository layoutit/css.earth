# Kepler-186 credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from G. Torres et al., "Validation of Twelve Small Kepler Transiting Planets in the Habitable Zone", ApJ 800, 99 (2015), arXiv:1501.01101. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
