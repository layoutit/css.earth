# Luhman 16 B credits

Maps: the posterior mean and variance maps of Ureshino, Y., Kawahara, H., Yama, H. and Masuda, K. (2026), "Bayesian Doppler Imaging: Simultaneous Inference of Surface Maps and Geometric Parameters", arXiv:2605.08544, from their frozen release prvjapan/BayesianDI v1.0.0, Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.20024778 (MIT license, copyright the authors), read unchanged. The spectra are the VLT/CRIRES observations of Crossfield, I. J. M., et al. (2014), "A global cloud map of the nearest known brown dwarf", Nature 505, 654, doi:10.1038/nature12955 (ESO). HEALPix pixel geometry: astropy-healpix.

Radius and temperature: Filippazzo, J. C., et al. (2015), ApJ 810, 158, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/810/2/158. Mass and orbit: Bedin, L. R., et al. (2024), Astronomische Nachrichten 345, e20230158, doi:10.1002/asna.20230158. Orbit check: Garcia, E. V., et al. (2017), ApJ 846, 97, doi:10.3847/1538-4357/aa844f.

Colour: the resolved Magellan/MagE optical spectrum of Luhman 16 B from Faherty, J. K., et al. (2014), "Signatures of Cloud, Temperature, and Gravity From Spectra of the Closest Brown Dwarfs", ApJ 790, 90, doi:10.1088/0004-637X/790/2/90, via the SIMPLE archive (SIMPLE-AstroDB, BSD-3-Clause, doi:10.5281/zenodo.13937301), through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).
