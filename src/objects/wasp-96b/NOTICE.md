# WASP-96 b credits

Radius: Hellier et al. 2014 (2014MNRAS.440.1982H), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Hellier et al. 2014, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2014MNRAS.440.1982H), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Hellier et al. 2014 (2014MNRAS.440.1982H), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,569 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
