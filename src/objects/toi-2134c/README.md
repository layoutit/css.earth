## Known problems

- **Orbit convention.** omega 160 degrees is taken as Rescigno et al. 2026 gives it through the archive (pl_orblper); papers differ on whether that is the star's or the planet's argument of periastron. The epoch is the transit, so a swapped convention would only mirror the ellipse about the line of sight.
