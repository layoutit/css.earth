# GJ 3090

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Lamontagne et al. 2026. It is also HIP 6365. This account was drafted from Lamontagne et al. 2026's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 4933912198895807104, parallax 44.535 ± 0.027 mas (22.45 pc). Radius 0.516 +/- 0.016 solar radii from Lamontagne et al. 2026, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of GJ 3090 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2026A&A...706A.278L/abstract). Mass 0.519 +/- 0.013 solar masses from Lamontagne et al. 2026, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of GJ 3090 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2026A&A...706A.278L/abstract). Temperature 3,707 K from Lamontagne et al. 2026, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of GJ 3090 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.73 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 4933912198895807104, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc188. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,707 K and log g 4.73 (u1 0.397, u2 0.357): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Not shown.** GJ 3090 c: found without a transit, and no paper's row measures its whole orbit together (period, eccentricity, periastron time and an inclination with an error bar) (found by radial velocity).
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
