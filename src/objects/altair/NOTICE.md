# Altair credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Monnier, Zhao, Pedretti et al., "Imaging the Surface of Altair", Science 317, 342 (2007), arXiv:0706.0867. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
