# Kepler-16 A credits

Radius, mass, effective temperature and the barycentre radial velocity: L. R. Doyle et al., "Kepler-16: A Transiting Circumbinary Planet", Science 333, 1602 (2011), arXiv:1109.3432, Table 1. Position, parallax and proper motion: Gaia EDR3 via SIMBAD (2020yCat.1350....0G). Only published numbers are used; the papers are not redistributed.

Numerical extraction and sphere: cssEarth, MIT. The neutral gray is a display convention, not an observed colour.
