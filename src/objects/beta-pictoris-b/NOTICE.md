# Beta Pictoris b credits

Shape: a sphere of the model radius (Morzinski et al. 2015); no image resolves the planet.

Orbit and mass: Lacour et al. (2021), A&A 654, L2, GRAVITY astrometry and HARPS radial velocities in the orbitize! conventions of Blunt et al. (2020).

Rotation: Zhou et al. (2026), arXiv:2607.13133 (period from JWST/NIRCam photometry, programme 4758) and Kraus et al. (2020), ApJ 897, L8 (spin aligned with the orbit). Radio: Ortiz Ceballos, Berger and Cendes (2026), arXiv:2609.16720, MeerKAT (SARAO).

Placement: the star Beta Pictoris at its Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Males et al. (2014), as compiled in Best, Liu, Magnier & Dupuy (2024), The UltracoolSheet v2.1 (Zenodo); zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (MKO K 2.184 µm, MKO H 1.614 µm, MKO J 1.2417 µm).

Limb darkening: Claret, Hauschildt & Witte (2012), A&A 546, A14, CDS J/A+A/546/A14.
