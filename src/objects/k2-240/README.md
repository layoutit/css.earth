# K2-240

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Díez Alonso et al. 2018. This account was drafted from Díez Alonso et al. 2018's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 6257625719430982016, parallax 13.691 ± 0.024 mas (73.04 pc). Radius 0.54 +/- 0.01 solar radii from Díez Alonso et al. 2018, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of K2-240 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2018MNRAS.480L...1D/abstract). Mass 0.58 +/- 0.01 solar masses from Díez Alonso et al. 2018, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of K2-240 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2018MNRAS.480L...1D/abstract). Temperature 3,810 K from Díez Alonso et al. 2018, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of K2-240 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.74 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** A Planck spectrum at 3,810 K, because no archive holds a spectrum of this star (stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number), through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffcf9c. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; gaia-xp: Gaia DR3 published no sampled BP/RP spectrum of it; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; planck: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 3,810 K and log g 4.74 (u1 0.422, u2 0.329): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Drafted text.** The card and introduction were written by the generator from the cited values, not by a person.
