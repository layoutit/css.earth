# Deneb credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from Aufdenberg, Hauschildt, Baron et al., "The Spectral Energy Distribution and Mass-loss Rate of the A-Type Supergiant Deneb", ApJ 570, 344 (2002), arXiv:astro-ph/0201218. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
