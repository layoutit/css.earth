# WASP-19 b credits

Radius: Cortés-Zuleta et al. 2020 (2020A&A...636A..98C), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Cortés-Zuleta et al. 2020, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2020A&A...636A..98C), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Cortés-Zuleta et al. 2020 (2020A&A...636A..98C), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,346 K dayside brightness temperature of Anderson et al. 2013, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
