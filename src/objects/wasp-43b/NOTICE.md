# WASP-43b credits

Temperature map and light curves: R. C. Challener, Z. Rustamkulov, E. K. H. Lee et al., "Latitudinal Asymmetry in the Dayside Atmosphere of WASP-43b", ApJL 969, L32 (2024), doi:10.3847/2041-8213/ad5958; data deposit "Latitudinal Asymmetry in the Dayside Atmosphere of WASP-43b", Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.12627524, CC BY 4.0, used unchanged. Based on observations made with the NASA/ESA/CSA James Webb Space Telescope, NIRSpec program 1224 (PI S. Birkmann), obtained from the Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes (doi:10.17909/n3ac-1s50). The colours are false colour chosen here (the plasma palette); the values are the authors'.

Radius and orbit: Challener et al. (2024), Table 1. Tidal locking is assumed, as in that paper.
