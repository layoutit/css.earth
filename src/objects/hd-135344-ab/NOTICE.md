# HD 135344 Ab credits

Orbit: the posterior of Stolker et al. (2025), arXiv:2507.06206, distributed by whereistheplanet (Wang et al. 2021), with the astrometry stored in it. Radius, temperature and mass: Stolker et al. (2025).

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Placement: its star at Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of Stolker et al. (2025), arXiv:2507.06206; zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (SPHERE K2 2.253 µm, SPHERE K1 2.102 µm, SPHERE H3 1.666 µm).

Limb darkening: Claret, Hauschildt & Witte (2012), A&A 546, A14, CDS J/A+A/546/A14.
