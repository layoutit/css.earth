# Beta Pictoris d credits

Shape: a sphere of the model radius (Sutlieff et al. 2026); no image of the planet exists. The gray is a display convention.

Orbit and mass: Sutlieff, Bonse et al. (2026), arXiv:2606.23801: the published semi-major axis, inclination and node, with a circular display orbit phased to their six measured positions.

Rotation: none measured; the display axis is the orbit normal.

Placement: the star Beta Pictoris at its Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).
