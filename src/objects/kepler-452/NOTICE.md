# Kepler-452 credits

Shape: a sphere of the radius from J. M. Jenkins et al., "Discovery and Validation of Kepler-452b: A 1.6 R⊕ Super Earth Exoplanet in the Habitable Zone of a G2 Star", AJ 150, 56 (2015), arXiv:1507.06723. No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity with the references SIMBAD lists. No measured rotation axis is adopted: the display axis is celestial north.
