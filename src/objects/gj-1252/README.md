# GJ 1252

## Sources

Its radius and temperature follow Shporer et al. 2020. The introduction is generated from Shporer et al. 2020's published values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 6468968316900356736, parallax 49.055 ± 0.025 mas (20.39 pc). Radius 0.391 +/- 0.02 solar radii from Shporer et al. 2020, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of GJ 1252 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020ApJ...890L...7S/abstract). Mass 0.381 +/- 0.019 solar masses from Shporer et al. 2020, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of GJ 1252 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020ApJ...890L...7S/abstract). Temperature 3,458 K from Shporer et al. 2020, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of GJ 1252 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.83 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 6468968316900356736, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffc98b. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: no HD number in SIMBAD; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret (2017), A&A 600, A30 computes from PHOENIX model atmospheres for the TESS band at 3,458 K and log g 4.83 (u1 0.169, u2 0.434): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
- **Model limb.** The limb darkening is a model atmosphere at the catalogued temperature and gravity, not a measurement of this star.
- **Quoted text.** The introduction quotes sentences of the Wikipedia article "GJ 1252" (revision 1367392674) verbatim, CC BY-SA 4.0.
