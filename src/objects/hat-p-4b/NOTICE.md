# HAT-P-4 b credits

Radius: Torres et al. 2008 (2008ApJ...677.1324T), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Torres et al. 2008, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2008ApJ...677.1324T), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Torres et al. 2008 (2008ApJ...677.1324T), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,194 K dayside brightness temperature of Todorov et al. 2013, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
