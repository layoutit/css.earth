# Kepler-13 b credits

Radius: Esteves et al. 2015 (2015ApJ...804..150E), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Esteves et al. 2015, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2015ApJ...804..150E), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Esteves et al. 2015 (2015ApJ...804..150E), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,750 K dayside brightness temperature of Shporer et al. 2014, the temperature of a black body showing the same occultation depths in all four bands, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
