# Kepler-16 (AB) b credits

Radius, mass, orbit size, tilt, eccentricity and phase: L. R. Doyle et al., "Kepler-16: A Transiting Circumbinary Planet", Science 333, 1602 (2011), arXiv:1109.3432, Table 1. Mean period: the Villanova Kepler Eclipsing Binary Catalogue (Kirk et al. 2016, AJ 151, 68), KIC 12644769. Only published numbers are used; nothing is redistributed.

Numerical extraction and sphere: cssEarth, MIT. The neutral gray is a display convention, not an observed colour.
