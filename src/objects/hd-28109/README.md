Its radius and temperature follow Barone et al. 2025. It is also HIP 20295. This account was drafted from Barone et al. 2025's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 4668163021600295552, parallax 7.130 ± 0.010 mas (140.24 pc). Radius 1.425 +/- 0.048 solar radii from Barone et al. 2025, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of HD 28109 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025A&A...704A..41B/abstract). Mass 1.227 +/- 0.052 solar masses from Barone et al. 2025, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of HD 28109 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025A&A...704A..41B/abstract). Temperature 6,189 K from Barone et al. 2025, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of HD 28109 b in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.22 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 4668163021600295552, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #f7f3ff. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 28109 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 6,189 K and log g 4.22 (u1 0.385, u2 0.301): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.

## Known problems

- **Assumptions of the frame.** The axis's position angle and the rotation phase are conventions.
