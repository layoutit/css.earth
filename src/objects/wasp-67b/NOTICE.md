# WASP-67 b credits

Radius: Stassun et al. 2017 (2017AJ....153..136S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Stassun et al. 2017, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2017AJ....153..136S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Stassun et al. 2017 (2017AJ....153..136S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Colour: a black body at the 1,015 K dayside brightness temperature of Kammer et al. 2015, dayside brightness temperature at 4.5 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions.
