# Antares credits

Shape: a sphere of the limb-darkened diameter measured by Ohnaka, Hofmann, Schertl et al., "High spectral resolution imaging of the dynamical atmosphere of the red supergiant Antares in the CO first overtone lines with VLTI/AMBER", A&A 555, A24 (2013). No image of the photosphere is shown; the neutral gray is a display convention.

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, proper motion and parallax from van Leeuwen, A&A 474, 653 (2007); radial velocity from Gontcharov, Astronomy Letters 32, 759 (2006). Mass for the display GM: Ohnaka et al. (2013). No measured rotation axis: the display axis is celestial north.

Colour: a Planck spectrum at the temperature of Effective temperature 3660 ± 120 K from Ohnaka et al. 2013 (A&A 555, A24; https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.4800), abstract: the bolometric flux with the VLTI/AMBER limb-darkened diameter 37.38 mas, the radius source., through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Limb darkening: Neilson & Lester (2013), A&A 554, A98, via VizieR J/A+A/554/A98.
