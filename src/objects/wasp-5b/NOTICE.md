# WASP-5 b credits

Radius: Gillon et al. 2009 (2009A&A...496..259G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Gillon et al. 2009, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2009A&A...496..259G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Gillon et al. 2009 (2009A&A...496..259G), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 2,996 K dayside brightness temperature of Chen et al. 2014, dayside brightness temperature at 1.22 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
