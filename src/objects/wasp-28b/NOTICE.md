# WASP-28 b credits

Radius: Mo&#x10D;nik et al. 2020 (2020PASP..132a4401M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Mo&#x10D;nik et al. 2020, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2020PASP..132a4401M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Mo&#x10D;nik et al. 2020 (2020PASP..132a4401M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 1,548 K dayside brightness temperature of Deming et al. 2023, dayside brightness temperature at 4.5 µm (uniform reanalysis of Spitzer's eclipses, CDS J/AJ/165/104 table 2), through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
