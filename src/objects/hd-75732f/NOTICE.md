# 55 Cnc f credits

Radius: the NASA Exoplanet Archive's calculated radius (its Chen & Kipping 2017 mass-radius relationship): a model, not a measurement, since 55 Cnc f does not transit. Mass: Moutou et al. (2026), A&A 705, A190, Table 3: minimum mass (m sin i) 48.5 +/- 2.8 Earth masses from the star's radial velocities; at the assumed tilt of 93.9 degrees the mass is 0.2% more.

Orbit: Moutou et al. (2026), A&A 705, A190, Table 3, a fit of the star's radial velocities (a/R* is its 0.802 au over the star's recorded 656,045 km); the orbit's plane is assumed, from Xiao et al. (2026, arXiv:2609.01377).
