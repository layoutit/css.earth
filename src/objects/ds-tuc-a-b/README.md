It is the only planet known around DS Tuc A. Its orbit and size follow Newton et al. 2019's fit, the archive's default. This account was drafted from Newton et al. 2019's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Size and mass.** Radius 0.50851994 Jupiter radii from Newton et al. 2019 (2019ApJ...880L..17N), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019ApJ...880L..17N/abstract): 36,355.1 km at 71,492 km per Jupiter radius. No mass is measured: Benatti et al. 2019 (2019A&A...630A..81B), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019A&A...630A..81B/abstract) gives only an upper limit of 1.3 Jupiter masses, so GM is 0, the records' unpublished value. A sphere: no oblateness is measured.

**Colour.** No image or measured colour exists. The neutral gray is lit by ds-tuc-a's measured colour (#ffefeb, the colour lens of ds-tuc-a (src/objects/ds-tuc-a/source/photometry/stellar-color.json)) at the gray's own brightness.
