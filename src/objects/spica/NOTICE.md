Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 7.47 +/- 0.54 solar radii from Tkachenko et al. (2016), MNRAS 458, 1964, Table 7: primary radius 7.47 +/- 0.54 solar radii from the combined light- and radial-velocity-curve solution (https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw255); Mass 11.43 +/- 1.15 solar masses from Tkachenko et al. (2016), MNRAS 458, 1964, Table 7: dynamical mass of the primary 11.43 +/- 1.15 solar masses (https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw255); temperature from Tkachenko et al. (2016), MNRAS 458, 1964, Tables 4 and 7: primary Teff 25,300 +/- 500 K from the disentangled spectrum.

Color: a Planck spectrum at the temperature of Tkachenko et al. (2016), MNRAS 458, 1964, Tables 4 and 7: primary Teff 25,300 +/- 500 K from the disentangled spectrum, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).
