Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 2.639 solar radii from McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770, table 2, HIP 105140: radius 2.639 solar radii, implied by the fitted luminosity 31.77 solar luminosities (fractional uncertainty 0.283) and temperature (https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stx1433); No mass is measured, so GM is 0, the records' unpublished value; temperature from McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770, table 2, HIP 105140: effective temperature 8442 +/- 1273 K, from the fit of a model atmosphere to the star's spectral energy distribution (goodness of fit Q 0.052).

Color: Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 6784024126773616128, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Placement: Gaia DR3 source 6784024126773616128: position, proper motion and radial velocity; distance: McDonald, Zijlstra & Watson (2017), MNRAS 471, 770, table 2, HIP 105140: distance 55.866 pc, the paper's parallax inverted (Gaia DR1's where it revised the star's, else the Hipparcos reduction of van Leeuwen 2007; section 2.3; fractional uncertainty 0.013), at which the luminosity and radius hold.
