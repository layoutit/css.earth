# 55 Cnc c credits

Radius: the NASA Exoplanet Archive's calculated radius (its Chen & Kipping 2017 mass-radius relationship): a model, not a measurement, since 55 Cnc c does not transit. Mass: Moutou et al. (2026), A&A 705, A190, Table 3: minimum mass (m sin i) 56.6 +/- 2.2 Earth masses from the star's radial velocities; at the assumed tilt of 93.9 degrees the mass is 0.2% more.

Orbit: Moutou et al. (2026), A&A 705, A190, Table 3, a fit of the star's radial velocities, for the period, eccentricity, argument, time of conjunction and semi-major axis (a/R* is its 0.247 au over the star's recorded 656,045 km). The tilt and node are NOT measured for this planet: they are assumed equal to those Xiao et al. (2026, arXiv:2609.01377) measure for 55 Cnc d.
