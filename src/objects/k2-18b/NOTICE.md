# K2-18 b credits

Radius, period, transit time and inclination: Benneke et al., "Water Vapor and Clouds on the Habitable-Zone Sub-Neptune Exoplanet K2-18b", ApJL 887, L14 (2019), arXiv:1909.04642, Table 2. Eccentricity and argument of periastron: Sarkis, Henning, Kürster et al., "The CARMENES search for exoplanets around M dwarfs: A low-mass planet in the temperate zone of the nearby K2-18", AJ 155, 257 (2018), arXiv:1805.00830, Table 3. Only published numbers are used; the papers are not redistributed.
