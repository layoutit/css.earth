# ε Eridani b credits

Radius: NASA Exoplanet Archive calculated radius from the mass, the Chen & Kipping (2017) relation: a model, not a measurement, since ε Eridani b does not transit. Mass: Thompson et al. (2025), AJ 170, 301, Table 3, the model with every data set: 1.00 +/- 0.10 Jupiter masses.

Orbit: Thompson et al. (2025), AJ 170, 301, Table 3, the model with every data set (RV, Hipparcos, FGS, Gaia DR2 and DR3): P 7.33 +0.08/-0.07 yr, a 3.53 +/- 0.04 au (over the host's recorded 0.74 solar radii), i 40 +6/-5 degrees, position angle of the ascending node 186 +8/-9 degrees. Drawn circular: e 0.06 +0.06/-0.04 is small and its omega and periastron time are each unconstrained (268 +62/-105 degrees; MJD 58017 +1740/-447), so their medians do not phase the orbit. The phase is the paper's section 4 statement that the planet is at its greatest separation ('opposition'), south of the star, on 2024-12-07 + N x 3.66 yr, moving counterclockwise: inferior conjunction MJD 59981.7 puts it at 1,096 mas and position angle 186 degrees on that date (their radial velocities alone give 1,071 +/- 58 mas)..
