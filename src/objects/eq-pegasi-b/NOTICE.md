Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 0.25 solar radii from Morin et al., arXiv:0808.1423, Table 1: radius 0.25 solar radii (theoretical radius; R sin i = 0.23(1)) (https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1423); Mass 0.25 solar masses from Morin et al., arXiv:0808.1423, Table 1: mass 0.25 solar masses (https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1423); temperature from Pecaut & Mamajek (2013, ApJS 208, 9), the online table's version 2022.04.16, row M4V, for SIMBAD's spectral type M4.0Ve: Teff. Color: a Planck spectrum at the temperature of Pecaut & Mamajek (2013, ApJS 208, 9), the online table's version 2022.04.16, row M4V, for SIMBAD's spectral type M4.0Ve: Teff, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Limb darkening: Claret (2017), A&A 600, A30, via VizieR J/A+A/600/A30.

Placement: Gaia DR3 source 2824770686019004032: position, parallax, proper motion. This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). Identifiers: SIMBAD, CDS, Strasbourg.
