# Sirius B credits

Radius: Bond et al. (2017), ApJ 840, 70, section 8: radius 0.008098 +/- 0.000046 solar radii from model-atmosphere fits to STIS spectra and the absolute flux (internal error). Mass: Bond et al. (2017), ApJ 840, 70, Table 6: dynamical mass 1.018 +/- 0.011 solar masses. Temperature: Bond et al. (2017), ApJ 840, 70, section 8: Teff 25,369 +/- 46 K from model-atmosphere fits to STIS spectra (internal error).

Orbit: Bond et al. (2017), ApJ 840, 70, Table 4: relative visual orbit P 50.1284 yr, a 7.4957 arcsec, i 136.336, node 45.4, T0 1994.5715 (Besselian year), e 0.59142, omega 149.161 degrees for the companion (the star's is 180 degrees on); a over the adopted parallax 0.3789 arcsec is 19.783 au, in units of the Sirius record's radius, 1,191,189 km.

Colour: a Planck spectrum at the cited temperature through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).
