# Peacock credits

Radius, mass and temperature: Radius 4.72 +/- 0.32 solar radii from Computed here, not printed by a paper: 4.72 +/- 0.32 solar radii, from the limb-darkened angular diameter 0.8 +/- 0.05 mas of Hanbury Brown, Davis & Allen (1974), MNRAS 167, 121 (the Narrabri intensity interferometer; Code, Davis, Bless & Hanbury Brown (1976), ApJ 203, 417, Table 1, and the JMDC) and the Hipparcos parallax 18.24 +/- 0.52 mas (van Leeuwen 2007, XHIP) (https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/167.1.121); No mass is measured, so GM is 0, the records' unpublished value; temperature from Code, Davis, Bless & Hanbury Brown (1976), ApJ 203, 417, HD 193924: effective temperature 17880 +/- 680 K (Table 6), from the measured angular diameter and the star's flux from the ultraviolet to the infrared.
