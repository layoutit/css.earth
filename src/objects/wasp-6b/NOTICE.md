# WASP-6 b credits

Radius: McGruder et al. 2023 (2023ApJ...944L..56M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: McGruder et al. 2023, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2023ApJ...944L..56M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: McGruder et al. 2023 (2023ApJ...944L..56M), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Colour: a black body at the 1,235 K dayside brightness temperature of Kammer et al. 2015, dayside brightness temperature at 3.6 µm (NASA Exoplanet Archive emission table), through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions.
