# AF Lep b credits

Orbit: the posterior of Balmer et al. (2025), distributed by whereistheplanet (Wang et al. 2021), and their VLTI/GRAVITY astrometry (Table 2). Radius, temperature and mass: Balmer et al. (2025). Discovery: Franson et al. (2023), ApJL 950, L19.

Shape: a sphere of the model radius; no image resolves the planet.

Placement: its star at Gaia DR3 astrometry (see that package's credits).

Color: infrared false color from the flux densities of De Rosa et al. (2023); Mesa et al. (2023), as compiled in Best, Liu, Magnier & Dupuy (2024), The UltracoolSheet v2.1 (Zenodo); zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service (2MASS Ks 2.159 µm, MKO H 1.614 µm, MKO J 1.2417 µm).

Limb darkening: computed with PICASO 4.1 (Batalha et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 70) on Exo-REM cloudy (Charnay et al. 2018, ApJ 854, 172; 3.16 times solar metallicity, C/O 0.55) profiles (https://lesia.obspm.fr/exorem/YGP_grids/old_grids_2021/) and PICASO's resampled opacity database (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3759675). Model fit: Balmer et al. (2025), arXiv:2411.05917.
