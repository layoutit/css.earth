Its radius and temperature follow Barros et al. 2022. It is also HIP 17264. This account was drafted from Barros et al. 2022's values; the sections below are the data's own.

**Star.** Placement: Gaia DR3 source 4674216245427964416, parallax 25.582 ± 0.013 mas (39.09 pc). Radius 0.71 +/- 0.02 solar radii from Barros et al. 2022, the stellar radius of the default parameter set of HD 23472 d in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022A&A...665A.154B/abstract). Mass 0.67 +/- 0.03 solar masses from Barros et al. 2022, the stellar mass of the default parameter set of HD 23472 d in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022A&A...665A.154B/abstract). Temperature 4,684 K from Barros et al. 2022, the stellar temperature of the default parameter set of HD 23472 d in the NASA Exoplanet Archive. log g 4.56 from the mass and radius.

**Colour.** Gaia DR3 XP spectrum, source 4674216245427964416, through the CIE 1931 2° observer: #ffd6bf. Routes tried in order: stis-ngsl: HD 23472 is not in the library; pulkovo: no HR number; kiehling: no HR number; kharitonov: no HR number; burnashev: no HR (BS) number; gaia-xp: used.

**Limb.** The disc is dimmed toward the limb by the quadratic law Claret & Bloemen (2011), A&A 529, A75 compute from ATLAS model atmospheres for the Johnson V band at 4,684 K and log g 4.56 (u1 0.741, u2 0.050): a model, because no fit of this star's limb is used.
