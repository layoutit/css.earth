# WASP-43 credits

Shape: a sphere of the stellar radius assumed by Challener, Rustamkulov, Lee et al., "Latitudinal Asymmetry in the Dayside Atmosphere of WASP-43b", ApJL 969, L32 (2024), Table 1. No image of the photosphere is shown.

Colour: a Planck spectrum at the Gaia DR3 GSP-Phot effective temperature (Gaia Collaboration 2023, A&A 674, A1; Andrae et al. 2023, A&A 674, A27) through the CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions (CIE 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, doi:10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

Placement: SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg) position, parallax and proper motion from Gaia EDR3 (Gaia Collaboration 2021); radial velocity from Gaia DR2 (Gaia Collaboration 2018). This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). No measured rotation axis: the display axis is celestial north.
