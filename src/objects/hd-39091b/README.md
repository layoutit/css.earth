## Known problems

- **Orbit convention.** omega -30.1 degrees is taken as Damasso et al. 2020 gives it through the archive (pl_orblper); radial-velocity papers usually give the star's argument of periastron, and a swapped convention would turn the orbit half a turn in its plane.
