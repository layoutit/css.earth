# TOI-1518 b credits

Radius: Simonnin et al. 2025 (2025A&A...698A.314S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Mass: Simonnin et al. 2025, the mass the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite table adopts (2025A&A...698A.314S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Orbit: Simonnin et al. 2025 (2025A&A...698A.314S), via the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Color: a black body at the 3,237 K dayside brightness temperature of Cabot et al. 2021, dayside brightness temperature in the TESS band, assuming zero geometric albedo, through the CIE 1931 2° color-matching functions.
